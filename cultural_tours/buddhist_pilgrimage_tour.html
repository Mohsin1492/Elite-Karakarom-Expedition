About the Tour

Taxila: Begin your spiritual odyssey in Taxila, a UNESCO World Heritage site and a cradle of Buddhist civilization. Explore the magnificent ruins, including the Dharmarajika Stupa, Taxila Museum, and the ancient monastic complexes. Feel the presence of the past in every stone.

Peshawar: Dive into the bustling city of Peshawar, where history and modernity blend seamlessly. Visit the historic Qissa Khawani Bazaar, experience the tranquility of the ancient Buddhist stupa, and uncover the fascinating stories that Peshawar has to offer.

Swat Valley: Journey to the enchanting Swat Valley, often called the "Switzerland of Pakistan." Here, discover the remains of Udegram Buddhist Monastery and marvel at the serene beauty of the valley that once served as a refuge for Buddhist monks.

Kalam: Explore Kalam, a paradise nestled in the heart of the Swat Valley. Take in the lush green landscapes, the soothing sound of the Ushu River, and immerse yourself in the serenity of this mountainous retreat.

Mingora: Conclude your pilgrimage in Mingora, the gateway to the Swat Valley. Reflect on your journey as you explore the local culture and take in the panoramic views of this beautiful valley.

The Journey

The tour begins in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, moving through the UNESCO heritage site of Taxila and the historic city of Peshawar. It then continues into the breathtaking Swat Valley, covering Kalam and Mingora before returning to Islamabad.

The journey concludes with a departure from Islamabad, leaving travelers with a profound spiritual experience and a deeper understanding of Buddhism's legacy in Pakistan.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy guided spiritual and historical tours, museum visits, stupa explorations, bazaar walks, and photography at some of the most significant Buddhist heritage sites in South Asia.

Travelers will also experience the stunning natural beauty of Swat and Kalam, along with the local culture, cuisine, and traditions of each region visited.

Detailed Itinerary (7 Days)

  • Day 1: Islamabad and Rawalpindi → Arrival, welcome & city orientation
  • Day 2: Islamabad → Taxila and Rawalpindi → Visit ruins & museum
  • Day 3: Drive from Islamabad to Peshawar → City exploration
  • Day 4: Drive from Peshawar to Swat → Arrive & sightseeing
  • Day 5: Visit Swat → Kalam & Mingora → Cultural & natural exploration
  • Day 6: Drive from Swat to Islamabad
  • Day 7: Transfer to Airport → Fly back Home
