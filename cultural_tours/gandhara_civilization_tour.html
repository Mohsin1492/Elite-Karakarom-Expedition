Gandhara & Indus Valley Civilization Tour

Explore the Legacy of Ancient Civilizations

Embark on a journey through time as we explore the ancient civilizations of Gandhara and the Indus Valley. From the serene capital of Islamabad to the historic streets of Peshawar, Lahore, and Karachi, this tour offers a deep cultural and historical experience.

Discover UNESCO World Heritage Sites, ancient Buddhist monasteries, Mughal architecture, and the remarkable ruins of Mohenjo-Daro—one of the oldest urban settlements in the world.

About the Tour

This tour is designed for history lovers, researchers, and travelers who want to explore Pakistan’s ancient civilizations and cultural heritage.

From Gandhara’s Buddhist history to the Indus Valley’s ancient urban planning, this journey offers a deep understanding of early human civilizations.

The Journey

The tour begins in Islamabad and moves through Swat and Peshawar, covering the Gandhara civilization. It then continues to Lahore for Mughal heritage and finally to Sindh, where the Indus Valley Civilization flourished.

The journey concludes in Karachi after exploring historical cities and archaeological sites.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy guided historical tours, museum visits, cultural exploration, sightseeing, and photography at some of the most significant heritage sites in South Asia.

Travelers will also experience local markets, traditions, and cuisine across different regions of Pakistan.

Detailed Itinerary (13 Days)

  • Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad → Welcome & hotel transfer
  • Day 2: Islamabad → Drive to Swat Valley
  • Day 3: Explore Swat Valley → Museums & cultural sites
  • Day 4: Swat → Drive to Peshawar
  • Day 5: Peshawar → Explore city, museum & bazaars → Return to Islamabad
  • Day 6: Islamabad → Drive to Lahore
  • Day 7: Explore Lahore → Mughal heritage sites
  • Day 8: Flight to Karachi
  • Day 9: Karachi → Drive to Hyderabad
  • Day 10: Hyderabad → Drive to Sukkur
  • Day 11: Sukkur → Visit Mohenjo-Daro → Return to Hyderabad
  • Day 12: Hyderabad → Drive to Karachi
  • Day 13: Transfer to Karachi Airport → Departure
