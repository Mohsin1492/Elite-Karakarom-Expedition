Shandur Polo Festival

Experience the World's Highest Polo Festival

Shandur Polo Festival is an amazing festival celebrated by the tribes of Gilgit and Chitral in the northern areas of Pakistan. The tribes from the regions of Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral meet at the Shandur Pass every summer, where a unique wild free-style polo is played between different teams of the region.

Shandur Polo Ground is considered to be the highest polo ground in the world at 3,700 meters, adjacent to the stunning Shandur Lake. The festival provides an amazing cultural experience in the Hindukush mountain ranges, offering travelers an unforgettable blend of sport, culture, and natural beauty.

About the Tour

This tour is designed for culture enthusiasts, adventure seekers, and travelers who want to witness one of Pakistan's most iconic and celebrated festivals. The Shandur Polo Festival brings together tribes, traditions, music, and sport in one of the most breathtaking settings on earth.

Beyond the festival, the tour covers some of Pakistan's most spectacular landscapes — from the Kalash Valley of Chitral to the majestic peaks of Hunza and the lush valleys of Naran and Kaghan.

The Journey

The tour begins with arrival in Islamabad, then moves through the Chitral Valley, Bumburet, and Mastuj before reaching the iconic Shandur Pass for the festival. It then continues through Khalti, Hunza, Khunjerab National Park, and Passu before returning via Naran to Islamabad.

The journey concludes with a departure from Islamabad, carrying memories of an extraordinary cultural and natural experience across northern Pakistan.

Activities & Experience

Watch thrilling polo matches at the world's highest polo ground, enjoy live indigenous music and dance performances, explore remote mountain valleys, and interact with the warm and welcoming local communities of Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral.

Travelers will also enjoy stunning lake views, mountain passes, historic forts, and the remarkable landscapes of northern Pakistan throughout the journey.
