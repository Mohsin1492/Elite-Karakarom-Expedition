Sikh Yatra Tour in Pakistan

A Sacred Journey Through Sikh Heritage

The Sikh Yatra Tour in Pakistan is a remarkable journey that allows travelers to explore the sacred Sikh sites in the country, immersing themselves in the rich history, spirituality, and cultural heritage of the Sikh faith. This tour offers a unique opportunity to visit revered gurdwaras, pay homage to historical landmarks, and experience the warm hospitality of the local Sikh community.

The Sikh Yatra Tour in Pakistan is a profoundly spiritual pilgrimage that invites you to connect with the sacred Sikh sites, immerse yourself in the rich historical and cultural heritage, and witness the vibrant Sikh traditions that thrive in the country. This transformative journey promises a deeper understanding and appreciation of Sikhism as you embark on a path of self-discovery and spiritual growth.

About the Tour

This tour is designed for Sikh pilgrims, history lovers, and spiritual travelers who wish to explore Pakistan's most revered Sikh heritage sites. Each destination holds deep religious significance and offers an enriching cultural experience.

From the sacred Gurdwaras of Lahore to the birthplace of Guru Nanak Dev Ji in Nankana Sahib, this journey covers the most important landmarks of the Sikh faith in Pakistan.

The Journey

The tour begins in Islamabad, moving through Hasan Abdal and on to Lahore, covering key Sikh pilgrimage sites along the way. It then continues to Kartarpur and Nankana Sahib before concluding with sightseeing in Lahore.

The journey ends with a departure from Lahore, leaving travelers with a profound spiritual and cultural experience to cherish.

Activities & Experience

Participate in guided religious tours, Gurdwara visits, prayers, and cultural exploration at some of the most sacred Sikh sites in South Asia.

Travelers will also experience the local culture, traditions, and cuisine of the regions visited throughout the pilgrimage.

Detailed Itinerary (7 Days)

  • Day 1: Islamabad → Arrival, welcome & city orientation
  • Day 2: Islamabad → Hasan Abdal → Visit Gurdwara Panja Sahib
  • Day 3: Islamabad → Drive to Lahore → Check-in & evening visit
  • Day 4: Full day visit to Kartarpur → Gurdwara Darbar Sahib
  • Day 5: Visit Nankana Sahib → Gurdwara Janam Asthan & nearby sites
  • Day 6: Lahore Sightseeing → Mughal heritage & historical landmarks
  • Day 7: Transfer to Airport → Fly back Home
