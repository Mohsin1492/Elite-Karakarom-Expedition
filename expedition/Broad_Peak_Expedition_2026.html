Broad Peak Expedition 2026

Summit the Giant of Karakoram

The Broad Peak Expedition is a high-altitude mountaineering journey designed for experienced climbers seeking to summit one of the world’s great 8,000-meter peaks. Located in the Karakoram Range of Pakistan, Broad Peak rises to 8,047 meters.

This expedition demands endurance, technical awareness, and strong acclimatization. While considered less technical than nearby K2, it remains a serious high-altitude challenge requiring prior climbing experience above 6,000 meters.

Date Peak Status
20 June 2026 Broad Peak Guaranteed

Mountain Overview

  • Elevation: 8,047m (26,401 ft)
  • Location: Karakoram Range, Pakistan
  • Type: High-altitude mountaineering expedition
  • Difficulty: Extreme
  • Duration: 45–65 Days

About the Expedition

Broad Peak, also known as Falchan Kangri, stands above the Baltoro Glacier near K2. It is a preferred 8,000-meter objective for climbers who want to progress beyond trekking peaks into true high-altitude mountaineering.

The expedition is supported by experienced mountain logistics teams, including fixed-line installation, base camp services, and acclimatization planning. Climbers traverse glaciers, snowfields, and steep ridges leading toward the summit.

Expedition Highlights

  • Summit attempt of Broad Peak (8,047m)
  • Full support Base Camp logistics
  • Views of K2, Gasherbrum I & II
  • Traverse Baltoro Glacier & Concordia
  • High-altitude climbing camps (I, II, III)
  • Scenic flight from Islamabad to Skardu

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Pakistan where our team will welcomes you at Islamabad International Airport and transfer you to your hotel. After all members arrive, we conduct a detailed expedition briefing covering logistics, permits, and climb strategy.

  • Stay: 3/4 Star Hotel
  • Activity: Gear check, briefing, optional city visit

Day 2 – Flight to Skardu / Road Transfer

We aim for a scenic flight to Skardu (2,230m), offering stunning aerial views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram range. If weather disrupts flights, we travel by road along the Karakoram Highway to Chilas.

  • Highlights: Nanga Parbat views
  • Contingency: Drive (~8–9 hrs)

Day 3 – Skardu Preparation Day

A key day for permit processing and final arrangements. You can explore Skardu, hike to Kharpocho Fort, or visit local villages.

  • Focus: Acclimatization & gear organization

Day 4 – Drive to Askole

A 4×4 jeep journey through Shigar and Braldu valleys brings us to Askole (3,000m), the last inhabited village before entering the wilderness.

  • Drive: ~7 Hours
  • Highlights: Transition into Karakoram terrain

Day 5 – Trek to Jula

The trek begins as we leave Askole behind. Crossing dry terrain and glacial streams, we reach Jula Camp via Korofong oasis.

  • Elevation: 3,100m
  • Trek: ~5 hours

Day 6 – Trek to Paiju

Follow the Braldu River to Paiju (3,380m), the final green campsite before entering the Baltoro Glacier zone.

  • Trek: ~6 Hours
  • Views: First sight of Trango Towers

Day 7 – Trek to Khoburtse

Step onto the Baltoro Glacier and traverse rocky moraine to Khoburtse (3,760m). Towering granite peaks dominate the skyline.

  • Trek: ~7 Hours
  • Terrain: Glacier & moraine

Day 8 – Trek to Urdukas

A shorter climb leads to Urdukas (4,130m), a scenic campsite above the glacier with panoramic views.

  • Trek: ~4 Hours
  • Note: Last grassy campsite

Day 9 – Acclimatization Day

Rest and adapt to altitude. Optional short walks and photography around camp.

Day 10 – Trek to Goro II

Return to the glacier and move toward Goro II (4,250m), surrounded by iconic peaks like Masherbrum.

  • Trek: ~7 hours
  • Condition: Camp on glacier

Day 11 – Trek to Concordia

Reach Concordia (4,600m), the meeting point of major glaciers. Surrounded by 8,000m peaks including K2, Broad Peak, and Gasherbrums.

  • Trek: ~5 hours
  • Highlight: First full view of K2

Day 12 – Trek to Broad Peak Base Camp

Move along the Godwin-Austen Glacier to Base Camp (5,000m). Spectacular views of K2 and surrounding giants.

  • Trek: 3–4 hours
  • Stay: Full-service camping

Day 13 to 42 – Climbing Period

The main expedition phase begins.

Strategy Overview:

  • Acclimatization Rotations: Between Base Camp, Camp 1 (~6,000m), and Camp 2 (~6,400m)
  • Route: Standard West Ridge via snow, rock, and mixed terrain
  • High Camp: Camp 3 (~7,000m) near the Col
  • Summit Push: From Camp 3 via ridge traverse to the main summit (8,047m)

Support:

  • Full Base Camp services
  • Weather updates & communication
  • High-calorie expedition meals

Day 43 – Descent to Khoburtse

Begin return journey after cleanup. Trek down past Concordia toward Khoburtse.

  • Duration: 7–8 hours

Day 44 – Trek to Paiju

Continue descending along the Baltoro Glacier back to greener terrain.

  • Trek: ~5 hours

Day 45 – Trek to Jula

Leave glacier terrain completely and follow the river valley.

  • Trek: ~7 hours

Day 46 – Trek to Askole

Final trekking day. Celebrate with porters and staff in Askole village.

  • Trek: 3-4 hours

Day 43 – Descent to Khoburtse

Begin return journey after cleanup. Trek down past Concordia toward Khoburtse.

  • Duration: 7–8 hours

Day 47 – Drive to Skardu

Return to Skardu by jeep. Enjoy comfort after weeks in the mountains.

  • Drive: ~7 hours

Day 48 – Fly to Islamabad

Morning flight back to Islamabad. Free time for rest or shopping.

  • Contingency: Road travel if flight is delayed

Day 49 – Reserve / City Tour

Buffer day for delays or sightseeing in Islamabad (Faisal Mosque, viewpoints, markets).

Day 50 – Departure

Transfer to airport for your international flight.

End of expedition.
