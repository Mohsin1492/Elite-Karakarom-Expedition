Charakusa Valley

The Granite Heart of the Karakoram

The Charakusa Valley Expedition takes you into one of the most dramatic climbing regions in Pakistan. Known for its massive granite walls and untouched alpine environment, this valley is a paradise for rock climbers and mountaineers.

Located near Hushe village in Baltistan, Charakusa offers quick access to world-class peaks without long logistical approaches, making it ideal for both technical climbers and experienced trekkers.

Why Choose Charakusa Valley?

  • Budget-Friendly Expeditions: Many peaks below 6,000m require no peak permits or liaison officers.
  • Easy Access: Short approach trek of around 3 days from Hushe village.
  • Scenic Base Camps: Green meadows surrounded by granite towers and glaciers.
  • World-Class Climbing: Technical routes suitable for advanced climbers.

Notable Peaks in Charakusa Valley

  • K6 (7,281m): A massive and dominant ice and rock formation.
  • K7 (6,934m): Famous for its steep granite walls and technical routes.
  • Link Sar (7,041m): A highly challenging peak for elite climbers.
  • Drifika (6,650m): A sharp and beautiful snow pyramid.
  • Niza Peak (<6,000m): Ideal for accessible, no-permit climbs.

Approach & Trekking Route

The journey begins in Hushe, a small but important village in Baltistan known as the gateway to many Karakoram expeditions. From here, a scenic trek leads toward Charakusa Valley.

The route passes through alpine pastures, river crossings, and glacier moraines before reaching base camps surrounded by towering granite peaks and snow-covered summits.

Expedition Experience

Charakusa Valley offers a unique combination of technical climbing and wilderness exploration. Climbers can experience both rock and ice routes in a remote environment with minimal human presence.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Islamabad where our team welcomes you at the airport and transfers you to the hotel. Meet fellow climbers and attend a briefing covering expedition logistics, permits, and equipment checks.

  • Activity: Airport transfer & expedition briefing
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Dinner

Day 2 – Fly to Skardu / Drive to Chilas

Take a spectacular flight to Skardu with aerial views of the Himalayas and Karakoram. In case of flight cancellation, travel by road along the famous Karakoram Highway to Chilas.

  • Travel: Flight or Karakoram Highway drive
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 – Skardu Preparation Day

Complete official formalities and attend the Ministry of Tourism briefing. The remainder of the day can be spent exploring Skardu town and preparing loads for the trek.

  • Activity: Final expedition arrangements
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4 – Drive to Hushe Village

Travel through the scenic valleys of Baltistan toward Hushe, the final major settlement before entering the Charakusa wilderness.

  • Drive: 4×4 Jeep Journey
  • Stay: Camping / Guesthouse
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5 – Trek to Saicho

Begin trekking through green pastures and rocky trails to Saicho Camp, surrounded by dramatic mountain scenery.

  • Trek: Gentle acclimatization hike
  • Elevation: Approx. 3,330m
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 – Trek to K6 & K7 Base Camp

Ascend toward the Charakusa Glacier and establish Base Camp beneath the towering granite walls of K6 and K7.

  • Trek: Glacier approach
  • Stay: Full Service Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7 to 26 – Climbing Period

Twenty days are dedicated to climbing objectives around the Charakusa Valley, including K6, K7, Kapura Peak, and nearby granite towers. Climbers will carry out acclimatization rotations, route fixing, and summit attempts depending on weather and conditions.

  • Activity: Technical climbing & acclimatization
  • Stay: Base Camp / High Camps
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 27 to 28 – Return Trek to Hushe

Descend from Base Camp back through the Charakusa Valley to Hushe Village. Celebrate the successful expedition with the local Balti team.

  • Trek: Glacier descent
  • Stay: Camping / Guesthouse
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 29 – Drive to Skardu

Return to Skardu for rest, hot showers, and the official expedition debriefing with tourism authorities.

  • Travel: Jeep transfer
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 30 – Return to Islamabad / Besham

Fly back to Islamabad or begin the scenic road journey along the Indus River and Karakoram Highway.

  • Travel: Flight or road journey
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 31 – Contingency / Free Day

Reserved for weather delays or optional sightseeing around Islamabad if flights operate on time.

  • Activity: Flexible schedule
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 32 – Departure

Transfer to Islamabad International Airport for your onward international flight, concluding the K6 & K7 Expedition.

  • Activity: Airport transfer
  • Meals: Breakfast
