Gasherbrum II Expedition 2026

Your First Step Above 8,000m

Gasherbrum II is one of the most popular entry-level 8,000-meter peaks, offering a balanced combination of challenge and accessibility. Standing at 8,035 meters, it is the 13th highest mountain in the world.

This expedition takes climbers deep into the Karakoram, following the famous Baltoro Glacier route to Concordia and onward to Gasherbrum Base Camp.

Expedition Status 2026

Date Peak Status
20 June 2026 Gasherbrum II Guaranteed

Expedition Snapshot

  • Mountain: Gasherbrum II (K4)
  • Elevation: 8,035m
  • Ranking: 13th highest mountain
  • Location: Karakoram, Pakistan
  • Duration: 45–50 Days
  • Difficulty: Advanced (High Altitude)
  • Best Season: June – August
  • Base Camp: ~5,200m

Why Climb Gasherbrum II?

  • Ideal introduction to 8,000m mountaineering
  • Less technical compared to K2
  • Incredible Baltoro Glacier trekking route
  • Views of K2, Broad Peak & Gasherbrum I
  • High success rate compared to other peaks

Journey to Base Camp

The expedition begins in Islamabad, followed by a scenic flight to Skardu. After preparation, a jeep ride leads to Askole, the starting point of the trek.

The route follows the Braldu River and continues onto the Baltoro Glacier, passing through iconic camps like Paiju, Urdukas, and Goro II before reaching Concordia.

From Concordia, the journey continues to Gasherbrum Base Camp, surrounded by towering peaks and vast glaciers.

Climbing Route & Camps

  • Base Camp: 5,200m (shared with Gasherbrum I)
  • Camp I: 5,900m via icefall
  • Camp II: 6,400m (Banana Ridge)
  • Camp III: 7,200m summit staging point
  • Summit: 8,035m with panoramic Karakoram views

Why Choose Us?

  • Experienced local guides from Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Full logistical support from Islamabad to Base Camp
  • Focus on safety, acclimatization, and success rate
  • Responsible and sustainable expedition practices

Final Note

Gasherbrum II offers a rare opportunity to step into the world of extreme altitude climbing. With the right preparation and support, it becomes a life-changing achievement for any mountaineer.

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Islamabad where our team will welcome you at the airport and transfer you to the hotel. Once the group gathers, we conduct an expedition briefing covering permits, logistics, safety, and climbing preparations.

  • Overnight: 3/4 Star Hotel
  • Activities: Gear check, briefing & optional city tour

Day 2: Flight to Skardu or Karakoram Highway Drive

Take the scenic mountain flight to Skardu with spectacular aerial views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram peaks. If flights are cancelled due to weather, the journey continues by road via the Karakoram Highway to Chilas.

  • Key Sight: Nanga Parbat from the aircraft
  • Contingency: 8–9 hour drive to Chilas

Day 3: Final Preparations in Skardu

A buffer and acclimatization day in Skardu. While permits are finalized, trekkers can explore Kharpocho Fort, Balti villages, and local markets.

  • Acclimatization: Gentle walks around Skardu
  • Logistics: Final equipment checks & porter load packing

Day 4: Jeep Safari to Askole

Travel by 4×4 jeep through Shigar Valley and the Braldu Gorge to Askole, the final village before entering the remote Karakoram wilderness.

  • Elevation: 3,000m
  • Drive Time: Approx. 7 Hours
  • Highlight: Gateway to the Baltoro region

Day 5: Trek from Askole to Jula

Begin the expedition on foot through the Braldu Valley, crossing rocky terrain and river sections before reaching Jula Camp.

  • Elevation: 3,100m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours
  • Terrain: Dry trails & suspension bridges

Day 6: Trek to Paiju

Continue trekking along the Braldu River toward Paiju Camp, the final green campsite before entering the Baltoro Glacier.

  • Elevation: 3,380m
  • Trek Time: 6 Hours
  • Views: Trango Towers & Cathedral Peaks

Day 7: Paiju to Khoburtse

Step onto the Baltoro Glacier and trek across moraine terrain toward Khoburtse Camp, surrounded by dramatic granite walls and glacier scenery.

  • Elevation: 3,760m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours
  • Terrain: Rocky glacier moraine

Day 8: Trek to Urdukas

Continue along the Baltoro Glacier to Urdukas, one of the most scenic campsites in the region with direct views of the Trango and Cathedral groups.

  • Elevation: 4,130m
  • Trek Time: 4 Hours
  • Highlight: Last grassy campsite before higher camps

Day 9: Acclimatization at Urdukas

Rest day for acclimatization to help the body adjust to higher altitude before advancing deeper into the glacier system.

Day 10: Trek to Goro II

Trek further across the Baltoro Glacier with magnificent views of Masherbrum and Muztagh Tower before arriving at Goro II Camp.

  • Elevation: 4,250m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours
  • Conditions: Camping directly on glacier ice

Day 11: Goro II to Concordia

Reach Concordia, the famous junction of the Baltoro and Godwin-Austen glaciers, surrounded by K2, Broad Peak, and the Gasherbrum peaks.

  • Elevation: 4,600m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours
  • Highlight: Panoramic views of K2 & Gasherbrum Group

Day 12: Concordia to Gasherbrum Base Camp

Leave Concordia and head south across the Upper Baltoro and Abruzzi Glaciers toward the Gasherbrum Sanctuary, home to Gasherbrum I and II Base Camps.

  • Elevation: 5,150m
  • Trek Time: 4–5 Hours
  • Highlight: Views of Chogolisa & Gasherbrum Peaks
  • Accommodation: Full-service Base Camp

Days 13 – 37: Climbing Period

The climbing phase focuses on acclimatization rotations, fixed rope preparation, and summit attempts on Gasherbrum-II using the Southwest Ridge route.

  • Rotations: Camp 1, Camp 2 & Camp 3 acclimatization
  • Route: Southwest Ridge via glacier and snow slopes
  • Summit Push: Final ascent during a stable weather window
  • Support: Weather updates, communication & Base Camp services

Day 38: Descent to Khoburtse

After completing the expedition and clean-up operations, begin the descent back through the Baltoro Glacier toward Khoburtse or Urdukas.

  • Target Camp: Khoburtse / Urdukas
  • Duration: 7–8 Hours

Day 39: Trek to Paiju

Continue descending through the glacier moraine before returning to the greener surroundings of Paiju Camp.

  • Elevation: 3,380m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours

Day 40: Trek to Jula

Leave the glacier behind and follow the river valley trails back toward Jula Camp.

  • Elevation: 3,170m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours

Day 41: Trek to Askole

Complete the final trekking stage back to Askole village and celebrate the successful expedition with the porter and support team.

  • Elevation: 3,050m
  • Trek Time: 3–4 Hours

Day 42: Drive to Skardu

Return by jeep through the mountain valleys to Skardu for rest, showers, and recovery.

  • Drive Time: Approx. 7 Hours

Day 43: Fly to Islamabad

Take the return flight to Islamabad. In case of weather delays, the return journey continues by road via the Karakoram Highway.

Day 44: Reserve Day / Islamabad Sightseeing

Buffer day reserved for flight delays or sightseeing in Islamabad including Faisal Mosque and Daman-e-Koh viewpoint.

Day 45: International Departure

Transfer to Islamabad International Airport for your return flight home. End of the Gasherbrum-II Expedition.
