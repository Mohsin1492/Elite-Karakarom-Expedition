Gasherbrum I Expedition 2026 (Hidden Peak)

Summit the Hidden Peak

Gasherbrum I (Hidden Peak) is one of the most challenging 8,000-meter mountains in the Karakoram Range. At 8,080 meters, it offers a demanding mix of altitude, ice climbing, and remote expedition logistics.

This expedition takes you through the legendary Baltoro Glacier region, passing iconic peaks like Trango Towers, Masherbrum, and Broad Peak before reaching Gasherbrum Base Camp.

Expedition Snapshot

  • Mountain: Gasherbrum I (Hidden Peak)
  • Elevation: 8,080m (11th highest in the world)
  • Location: Karakoram Range, Pakistan
  • Duration: 45 Days
  • Difficulty: Very High (Technical + Altitude)
  • Best Season: June – August
  • Route: Northwest Face / Japanese Couloir

Why Climb Gasherbrum I?

  • One of the most remote 8,000m peaks in the world
  • Stunning Baltoro Glacier approach trek
  • 360° mountain views at Concordia
  • Views of K2, Broad Peak & Gasherbrum II
  • Less crowded compared to Everest & K2
  • Full logistical support up to Base Camp

About the Expedition

Gasherbrum I lies deep within the Karakoram and is considered a true alpinist’s peak. The journey begins in Islamabad and continues through Skardu, Askole, and the Baltoro Glacier, leading to one of the most remote Base Camps in the world.

Unlike standard trekking routes, this expedition requires advanced mountaineering skills, endurance, and prior experience above 6,500 meters.

Climbing Route & Camps

  • Base Camp: Shared with Gasherbrum II at ~5,000m
  • Camp I: 5,900m via icefall and Gasherbrum La
  • Camp II: 6,400m near Japanese Couloir
  • Camp III: 7,200m final summit staging point

Expedition Overview (45 Days)

  • Days 1–5: Islamabad → Skardu → Askole
  • Days 6–12: Baltoro Glacier trek to Concordia
  • Days 13–14: Arrival at Gasherbrum Base Camp
  • Days 15–37: Climbing rotations & summit attempt
  • Days 38–45: Return journey to Islamabad

Final Note

Gasherbrum I is a true test of endurance and skill. With extreme altitude, technical terrain, and remote conditions, it is designed for serious climbers seeking a world-class 8,000-meter experience.

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Islamabad where our team will receive you at the airport and transfer you to the hotel. After the group gathers, we conduct an expedition briefing covering permits, logistics, equipment checks, and the climbing plan ahead.

  • Overnight: 3/4 Star Hotel
  • Activities: Gear check, briefing & optional city visit

Day 2: Flight to Skardu or Karakoram Highway Drive

Take the scenic mountain flight to Skardu, offering incredible aerial views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram range. If weather conditions prevent flying, the journey continues by road along the famous Karakoram Highway to Chilas.

  • Key Sight: Nanga Parbat from the aircraft
  • Contingency: 8–9 hour drive to Chilas if flight is cancelled

Day 3: Preparations in Skardu

A preparation and acclimatization day in Skardu. While permits are finalized, trekkers can explore local attractions including Kharpocho Fort and nearby Balti villages.

  • Acclimatization: Light walking around Skardu
  • Logistics: Final packing and porter load preparation

Day 4: Jeep Drive to Askole

Travel by 4×4 jeep through the Shigar and Braldu valleys to Askole, the final permanent settlement before entering the Karakoram wilderness.

  • Elevation: 3,000m
  • Drive Time: Approx. 7 Hours
  • Highlight: Beginning of the remote mountain expedition

Day 5: Trek from Askole to Jula

The expedition officially begins on foot. Trek through the Braldu Valley, cross the Biafo Glacier region, and continue toward Jula Camp via suspension bridges and rugged terrain.

  • Elevation: 3,100m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours
  • Terrain: Dry trails, river crossings & rocky paths

Day 6: Trek to Paiju

Follow the Braldu River upstream toward Paiju Camp, the last green campsite before entering the Baltoro Glacier region.

  • Elevation: 3,380m
  • Trek Time: 6 Hours
  • Views: Trango Towers & Cathedral Peaks

Day 7: Paiju to Khoburtse

Step onto the Baltoro Glacier and trek through moraine terrain toward Khoburtse Camp, surrounded by dramatic granite towers.

  • Elevation: 3,760m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours
  • Terrain: Glacier moraine & rocky ice paths

Day 8: Trek to Urdukas

Continue along the glacier moraine to Urdukas, one of the most scenic campsites in the Baltoro region.

  • Elevation: 4,130m
  • Trek Time: 4 Hours
  • Highlight: Last grassy campsite before higher glacier camps

Day 9: Acclimatization at Urdukas

A rest and acclimatization day to help the body adjust to altitude before moving toward the higher glacier camps.

Day 10: Trek to Goro II

Trek deeper into the Baltoro Glacier system with views of Masherbrum and Muztagh Tower before reaching Goro II Camp.

  • Elevation: 4,250m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours
  • Conditions: Camping directly on glacier ice

Day 11: Goro II to Concordia

Reach Concordia, famously known as the “Throne Room of the Mountains,” where the Baltoro and Godwin-Austen glaciers meet beneath towering 8,000m peaks including K2.

  • Elevation: 4,600m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours
  • Highlight: Panoramic views of K2 & Gasherbrum Peaks

Day 12: Concordia to Gasherbrum Base Camp

Trek south across the Upper Baltoro and Abruzzi Glaciers toward the Gasherbrum Base Camp, surrounded by dramatic ice formations and giant Karakoram peaks.

  • Elevation: 5,150m
  • Trek Time: 4–5 Hours
  • Highlight: Direct views of Gasherbrum I & II
  • Accommodation: Full-service Base Camp

Days 13 – 37: Climbing Period

Multiple weeks dedicated to acclimatization rotations, route preparation, and summit attempts. Climbers move between higher camps while adapting to altitude and monitoring weather windows.

  • Rotations: Camp 1, Camp 2 & Camp 3 acclimatization
  • Route: Standard glacier and ridge climbing sections
  • Summit Push: Final ascent during stable weather conditions
  • Support: Meals, weather updates & communication assistance

Day 38: Descent to Khoburtse

After completing the expedition and clean-up operations, begin the descent back through the Baltoro Glacier toward Khoburtse.

  • Target Camp: Khoburtse / Urdukas
  • Duration: 7–8 Hours

Day 39: Trek to Paiju

Continue descending along the glacier moraine before returning to the greener surroundings of Paiju Camp.

  • Elevation: 3,380m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours

Day 40: Trek to Jula

Leave the glacier behind completely and follow the river valley trails back to Jula Camp.

  • Elevation: 3,170m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours

Day 41: Trek to Askole

Complete the final trekking stage back to Askole village and celebrate the successful expedition with the support team and porters.

  • Elevation: 3,050m
  • Trek Time: 3–4 Hours

Day 42: Drive to Skardu

Return by jeep through the mountain valleys to Skardu for rest, hot showers, and recovery.

  • Drive Time: Approx. 7 Hours

Day 43: Fly to Islamabad

Take the return flight to Islamabad. In case of weather delays, the return journey continues by road via the Karakoram Highway.

Day 44: Reserve Day / Islamabad Sightseeing

Buffer day reserved for weather delays or sightseeing in Islamabad, including Faisal Mosque and Daman-e-Koh viewpoint.

Day 45: International Departure

Transfer to Islamabad International Airport for your return flight home. End of the Gasherbrum-I Expedition.
