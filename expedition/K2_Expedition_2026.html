K2 Expedition 2026: Summit the Savage Mountain

Conquer the Savage Mountain

K2 stands at 8,611 meters, making it the second-highest mountain in the world and one of the most technically demanding climbs ever attempted. Known as the “Savage Mountain,” it challenges even the most experienced alpinists.

Located in the Karakoram Range, this expedition takes you deep into one of the most remote and breathtaking mountain regions on Earth.

Expedition Status 2026

Date Peak Status
20 June 2026 K2 (8,611m) Guaranteed

Expedition Snapshot

  • Mountain: K2 (Mount Godwin-Austen)
  • Elevation: 8,611m
  • Location: Karakoram Range, Pakistan
  • Duration: 60+ Days
  • Difficulty: Extreme (Technical + High Altitude)
  • Route: Abruzzi Spur (Southeast Ridge)

Expedition Highlights

  • Summit the second-highest mountain in the world
  • Climb via the legendary Abruzzi Spur
  • Experience the Baltoro Glacier approach
  • 360° views of Broad Peak and Gasherbrum peaks
  • Full base camp support and logistics

The Journey to Base Camp

The expedition begins in Islamabad, followed by a scenic flight to Skardu. After preparation, a jeep journey leads to Askole—the final village before the trek begins.

The trekking route follows the Braldu Valley and continues across the Baltoro Glacier, passing through camps like Paiju, Urdukas, and Goro II before reaching Concordia.

From Concordia, climbers proceed to K2 Base Camp, located at the foot of the mighty Godwin-Austen Glacier.

Base Camp Life

  • Heated dining and resting tents
  • High-energy meals prepared by professional cooks
  • Solar-powered charging facilities
  • Weather updates and communication systems

Climbing Route (Abruzzi Spur)

  • Camp I: Ridge climb over mixed terrain
  • Camp II: Includes technical rock section (House’s Chimney)
  • Camp III: Located above the Black Pyramid
  • The Shoulder: Final staging point (~7,900m)
  • The Bottleneck: Critical and dangerous ice section
  • Summit: 8,611m with unmatched Karakoram views

Climbing Legacy

K2 has a rich history of exploration and mountaineering. First summited in 1954, it remains one of the most respected and feared mountains in the world.

Every successful ascent represents not just physical strength, but mental resilience and precise decision-making.

Final Note

K2 is not just a climb—it is a defining achievement in the world of mountaineering. Only the most prepared and determined climbers should attempt this expedition.

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad

Touch down in Pakistan. Most international flights arrive in Islamabad early in the morning. Our team will meet you at the airport and transfer you to the hotel for rest and recovery. Once the group gathers, we conduct an expedition briefing covering logistics, permits, and climbing preparations.

  • Overnight: 3/4 Star Hotel
  • Activity: Gear check, briefing & optional city tour

Day 2: The Skardu Flight (or Karakoram Highway Drive)

We aim for the scenic flight to Skardu (2,230m), offering breathtaking aerial views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram range. If weather conditions prevent flying, we continue by road via the Karakoram Highway to Chilas.

  • Key Sight: Nanga Parbat visible from the aircraft
  • Contingency: Drive to Chilas (461km / 8–9 hrs) if flight is cancelled

Day 3: Final Preparations in Skardu

A buffer day for permit formalities and final expedition arrangements. Explore Skardu’s attractions including Kharpocho Fort and the Organic Village while the team finalizes logistics.

  • Acclimatization: Gentle walking around Skardu (2,230m)
  • Logistics: Final kit check & porter load packing

Day 4: Jeep Safari to Askole

Drive by 4×4 jeep through the Shigar Valley and Braldu Gorge to Askole (3,000m), the final permanent settlement before entering the Karakoram wilderness.

  • Elevation: 3,000m
  • Drive Time: ~7 Hours
  • Highlight: Entering the remote Karakoram region

Day 5: Askole to Jula (The Trek Begins)

The expedition begins on foot through the Braldu Valley. Cross the snout of the Biafo Glacier and continue to Jula Camp after crossing the Dumurdo River suspension bridge.

  • Elevation: 3,100m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours
  • Terrain: Dusty trails, rivers & dry valley paths

Day 6: Jula to Paiju

Follow the Braldu River upstream toward Paiju (3,380m), the final green campsite before reaching the Baltoro Glacier.

  • Elevation: 3,380m
  • Trek Time: 6 Hours
  • Key View: Trango Towers & Cathedral Peaks

Day 7: Paiju to Khoburtse (Entering the Glacier)

Trek onto the Baltoro Glacier across rocky moraine terrain and continue toward Khoburtse (3,760m), surrounded by dramatic granite walls.

  • Elevation: 3,760m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours
  • Terrain: Glacial moraine & rocky trails

Day 8: Khoburtse to Urdukas

Continue along the glacier moraine to Urdukas (4,130m), one of the most scenic campsites overlooking the Baltoro Glacier.

  • Elevation: 4,130m
  • Trek Time: 4 Hours
  • Significance: Last grassy campsite before ice terrain

Day 9: Acclimatization at Urdukas

A full rest and acclimatization day at Urdukas to help your body adapt to higher altitude before continuing deeper into the Karakoram.

Day 10: Urdukas to Goro II

Trek across the Baltoro Glacier toward Goro II (4,250m), surrounded by peaks like Masherbrum and Muztagh Tower.

  • Elevation: 4,250m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours
  • Conditions: Camping directly on glacier ice

Day 11: Goro II to Concordia (The Throne Room)

Arrive at Concordia (4,600m), the legendary junction of the Baltoro and Godwin Austen glaciers, with unforgettable views of K2, Broad Peak, and Gasherbrum IV.

  • Elevation: 4,600m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours
  • Highlight: First unobstructed views of K2

Day 12: Concordia to Broad Peak Base Camp

Continue along the Godwin Austen Glacier toward Broad Peak Base Camp (5,000m), an important staging area for climbers heading to Broad Peak and K2.

  • Elevation: 5,000m
  • Trek Time: 4 Hours

Day 13: Arrival at K2 Base Camp

Trek to K2 Base Camp (5,150m), passing the historic Gilkey Memorial along the route. This will serve as the team’s home for the coming weeks.

  • Elevation: 5,150m
  • Trek Time: 6 Hours
  • Objective: Establish Base Camp operations

Days 14 – 42: Climbing Period (The Abruzzi Spur)

Over the next 28 days, climbers complete acclimatization rotations between Base Camp and higher camps while preparing for the summit push on K2 via the Abruzzi Spur route.

  • Rotations: Camps 1, 2 & 3 for acclimatization
  • Route: Abruzzi Spur via House’s Chimney & Black Pyramid
  • Summit Push: Through the Bottleneck to 8,611m
  • Support: Meals, satellite weather & medical assistance

Day 43: Clean Up & Trek to Khoburtse

After expedition clean-up, begin the descent from Base Camp, trekking quickly down toward Khoburtse or Urdukas.

  • Target: Khoburtse (3,560m)
  • Duration: 7–8 Hours downhill

Day 44: Trek to Paiju

Continue descending along the Baltoro Glacier back to the green campsite of Paiju.

  • Elevation: 3,380m
  • Trek Time: 5 Hours

Day 45: Trek to Jula

Leave the glacier behind and follow the valley trails back to Jula Camp.

  • Elevation: 3,170m
  • Trek Time: 7 Hours

Day 46: Trek to Askole (Celebration Day)

Complete the final trek back to Askole village and celebrate the expedition with the porters and support team.

  • Elevation: 3,050m
  • Trek Time: 3–4 Hours

Day 47: Drive to Skardu

Return by jeep through the Braldu and Shigar valleys to Skardu for rest and recovery.

  • Drive Time: 7 Hours

Day 48: Fly to Islamabad

Take the return flight to Islamabad. In case of cancellation, continue by road along the Karakoram Highway.

  • Contingency: Road journey via KKH if flight is cancelled

Day 49: Reserve Day / Sightseeing

Buffer day for weather delays or optional sightseeing in Islamabad, including Faisal Mosque and Daman-e-Koh.

Day 50: International Departure

Transfer to Islamabad International Airport for your onward flight. End of the K2 Expedition.
