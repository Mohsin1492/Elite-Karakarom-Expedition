Laila Peak Expedition 2026

Climb the Iconic Needle of the Karakoram

Laila Peak stands at 6,096 meters and is widely known for its perfectly symmetrical shape and steep snow-covered slopes. It is one of the most beautiful and technically rewarding peaks in Pakistan.

This expedition offers climbers a chance to test their technical skills in a high-altitude environment while enjoying breathtaking views of the Karakoram’s legendary peaks.

Expedition Highlights

  • Climb one of the most iconic peaks in the Karakoram
  • Panoramic views of K2, Broad Peak, and Gasherbrum range
  • Experience the remote Hushe Valley
  • Scenic flight over Nanga Parbat
  • Perfect training climb before 7,000m peaks

Expedition Overview

Laila Peak is often considered a bridge between intermediate and advanced mountaineering. Its steep slopes require strong crampon technique, ice axe control, and high-altitude endurance.

The expedition duration ranges from 23 to 35 days depending on weather conditions and acclimatization progress.

The Journey

The expedition begins in Islamabad, followed by a flight to Skardu where final preparations are completed. From Skardu, a scenic drive leads to Hushe Valley, the gateway to many Karakoram expeditions.

The trek continues through Saicho and onto Laila Peak Base Camp, located at approximately 4,535 meters, surrounded by stunning alpine scenery.

The Climb

The ascent involves steep snow and ice slopes, typically around 40–45 degrees. Climbers must be comfortable using crampons, ropes, and ice tools in exposed conditions.

Our team provides full logistical support at Base Camp, including food, communication, and weather updates, while climbers execute the ascent.

Final Note

Laila Peak is not just a climb—it is a test of precision, balance, and confidence on steep terrain. It prepares climbers for bigger objectives while offering one of the most visually stunning summits in the world.

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad

Your journey begins in Pakistan’s capital. Upon arrival at Islamabad International Airport, our team will transfer you to the hotel. In the evening, we conduct an expedition briefing covering logistics, safety, and preparations for the climb ahead.

  • Stay: Hotel in Islamabad

Day 2: Aerial Reconnaissance – Flight to Skardu (2,230m)

Take the famous mountain flight to Skardu, offering unforgettable aerial views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram range. If flights are cancelled due to weather, we continue by road along the scenic Karakoram Highway.

  • Highlight: Aerial views of Nanga Parbat & K2 region
  • Contingency: Drive via Karakoram Highway (KKH)

Day 3: Heritage & Acclimatization in Skardu

A preparation and acclimatization day in Skardu. While permits are finalized, explore landmarks including Kharpocho Fort and the ancient Manthal Buddha Rock.

  • Acclimatization: Adjusting to 2,000m+ altitude
  • Highlights: Kharpocho Fort & Manthal Buddha Rock

Day 4: Skardu to Hushe Village (3,170m)

Drive through the Khaplu Valley along the Indus and Shyok Rivers toward Hushe Village, the final settlement before the high mountains. Masherbrum dominates the skyline above camp.

  • Elevation: 3,170m
  • Highlight: Views of Masherbrum (7,821m)

Day 5: Trek to Saicho (3,330m)

Begin trekking through the Hushe Valley on a gentle acclimatization walk to Saicho, a beautiful campsite and gateway to several major climbing routes.

  • Elevation: 3,330m
  • Trek Time: Approx. 5 Hours

Day 6: Arrival at Laila Peak Base Camp (4,535m)

Ascend toward the Gondogoro Glacier and establish Laila Peak Base Camp beneath the iconic needle-shaped summit of Laila Peak.

  • Elevation: 4,535m
  • Trek Time: 4–5 Hours
  • Highlight: Panoramic glacier & granite views

Days 7 – 17: Climbing Window

The climbing phase begins on the steep ice slopes of Laila Peak. The route combines mixed rock, snow, and sustained 55-degree ice climbing toward the summit ridge.

  • Route: Mixed terrain transitioning into steep ice
  • ABC: Advanced Base Camp around 5,765m
  • Support: Full Base Camp meals & logistics support

Days 18 – 19: Descent to Civilization

After the summit push, descend back through Saicho and Hushe while celebrating the successful expedition with local Balti porters and support staff.

Day 20: Return to Skardu via Khaplu

Drive back to Skardu with a stop at the historic Chaqchan Mosque in Khaplu. Enjoy the comfort of a hotel after days in the mountains.

  • Highlight: Visit to 14th-century Chaqchan Mosque

Days 21 – 23: Islamabad & International Departure

Fly back to Islamabad with contingency time for weather delays. Enjoy a farewell dinner before your transfer to Islamabad International Airport for departure.

  • Contingency: Extra buffer day for weather delays
  • Farewell: Traditional Pakistani dinner
