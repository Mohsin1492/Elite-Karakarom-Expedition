Nanga Parbat Expedition 2026

Conquer the Killer Mountain

Nanga Parbat rises to 8,126 meters, making it the 9th highest mountain in the world. Known as the “Killer Mountain,” it is famous for its extreme vertical relief and demanding climbing routes.

Located in the western Himalayas of Pakistan, this expedition offers a unique combination of technical climbing, remote wilderness, and breathtaking scenery.

About the Expedition

Nanga Parbat is known for its massive structure and technical difficulty. The Diamir Face, approached via the Kinshofer Route, is considered the standard path but still requires strong mountaineering skills.

This expedition is suitable only for experienced climbers who have previously climbed at high altitudes and are comfortable in extreme conditions.

The Journey

The expedition begins in Islamabad, followed by a scenic drive along the Karakoram Highway to Chilas. From there, the journey continues to the Diamir side of Nanga Parbat.

A short trek leads to Base Camp, where climbers prepare for acclimatization and the ascent.

Climbing Route & Camps

  • Base Camp: ~4,200m (Diamir Side)
  • Camp I: ~5,000m via rocky terrain
  • Camp II: ~6,100m (steep snow slopes)
  • Camp III: ~6,700m high camp
  • Summit: 8,126m via Kinshofer Route

Final Note

Nanga Parbat is not just a climb—it is a true test of endurance, courage, and technical ability. Only well-prepared climbers should attempt this legendary mountain.

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Islamabad where our team will receive you at the airport and transfer you to the hotel. After the group gathers, we conduct an expedition briefing covering permits, logistics, and climbing plans.

  • Overnight: 3/4 Star Hotel
  • Activity: Gear check, briefing & optional city tour

Day 2: The Karakoram Highway (Islamabad to Chilas)

Begin the legendary drive on the Karakoram Highway, passing through Taxila, Abbottabad, Besham, and Dassu before reaching Chilas in the Diamir region.

  • Drive: 480km (12–13 Hours)
  • Overnight: Hotel in Chilas

Day 3: Jeep Safari & Trek to Diamoroi Sier

Continue by 4×4 jeep from Chilas to Halaly Bridge. From here, begin the trek to Diamir Ser, a seasonal shepherd settlement surrounded by mountain scenery.

  • Drive: 2 Hours
  • Trek: 5 Hours
  • Overnight: Full-Service Camping

Day 4: Trek to Diamir Base Camp (4,100m)

Trek through birch forests and rocky valleys toward Diamir Base Camp. Stop at Kutagali for lunch before the final ascent to base camp.

  • Elevation: 4,100m
  • Trek: 5 Hours
  • Overnight: Base Camp

Days 5 – 35: Climbing Period & Acclimatization

The main climbing phase begins with acclimatization rotations between higher camps. Climbers follow the Kinshofer Route while the base camp team manages logistics, weather updates, and support.

  • Objective: Summit Nanga Parbat (8,126m)
  • Route: Kinshofer Route
  • Support: Weather, communication & medical assistance

Day 36: Base Camp Teardown & Trek Down

After the expedition, the team cleans the mountain, packs equipment, and descends back to Diamir Ser camp.

  • Activity: Trek to Diamir Ser
  • Overnight: Camping

Day 37: Return to Civilization

Trek back to Halaly Bridge and continue by jeep to Chilas. Enjoy a comfortable hotel stay after weeks in the mountains.

  • Activity: Trek & Drive to Chilas
  • Overnight: Hotel in Chilas

Day 38: Drive to Islamabad

Complete the long return journey along the Karakoram Highway back to Islamabad.

  • Drive: Approx. 12 Hours
  • Overnight: Hotel in Islamabad

Day 39: Official De-Briefing

Attend the official farewell meeting at the Alpine Club of Pakistan to complete expedition formalities. The rest of the day is free.

  • Activity: Farewell & permit closure
  • Overnight: Hotel in Islamabad

Day 40: International Departure

Transfer to Islamabad International Airport for your flight home, marking the end of the expedition.

  • Activity: Airport Transfer
  • End: Expedition Concludes
