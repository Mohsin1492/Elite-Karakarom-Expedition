Rakaposhi Expedition 2025-26

Climb the Shining Wall of the Karakoram

Rakaposhi rises to 7,788 meters and is one of the most striking peaks in Pakistan. It is famous for its massive vertical rise from the Nagar Valley, making it one of the greatest land elevations in the world.

Known as the “Mother of Mist,” this mountain offers climbers a combination of technical climbing, breathtaking scenery, and cultural exploration in the Hunza region.

Expedition Snapshot

  • Mountain: Rakaposhi
  • Elevation: 7,788m
  • Ranking: 27th highest mountain
  • Location: Nagar Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Duration: 35–40 Days
  • Route: Southwest Spur
  • Difficulty: High (Technical Climb)

Expedition Highlights

  • Experience one of the greatest vertical drops in the world
  • Climb via the classic Southwest Spur route
  • Explore the beautiful Hunza & Nagar valleys
  • Less crowded compared to 8000m expeditions
  • Views of Diran Peak and surrounding Karakoram ranges

About the Expedition

Rakaposhi offers a unique climbing experience that combines technical challenges with unmatched natural beauty. While not as crowded as the 8000-meter peaks, it requires strong physical fitness and mountaineering skills.

The Southwest Spur route is the most commonly used path, involving steep snow slopes, exposed ridges, and glacier travel.

The Journey

The expedition begins in Islamabad and follows the legendary Karakoram Highway to the Hunza region. From there, the journey continues to Minapin village, the gateway to Rakaposhi.

A scenic trek leads to Tagafari and Base Camp, surrounded by alpine meadows and dramatic mountain views.

Climbing Route & Camps

  • Base Camp: ~3,900m
  • Camp I: Glacier approach
  • Camp II: Steep snow slopes
  • Camp III: High ridge camp
  • Summit: 7,788m via Southwest Spur

Sustainable Expedition

We follow responsible tourism practices to protect the fragile mountain environment. Our team ensures proper waste management and supports local communities by hiring local guides and porters.

Final Note

Rakaposhi is a mountain that combines beauty with challenge. It is ideal for climbers seeking a serious expedition without the extreme crowds of higher peaks.

Detailed 40-Day Itinerary: The Road to Glory

Phase 1: The Approach to the North

The journey begins in Islamabad with an expedition briefing before traveling north on the legendary Karakoram Highway.

  • Days: 1 – 3
  • Route: Islamabad to Minapin
  • Highlights: Taxila, Besham & Chilas
  • Key View: First glimpse of Nanga Parbat

Minapin Village

Arrive at Minapin, a peaceful mountain village surrounded by orchards and the main gateway for the Diran expedition.

  • Location: Nagar Valley
  • Famous For: Orchards & mountain access

Phase 2: Trek to Base Camp

Leave the road behind and begin trekking through meadows, ridges, and glacier terrain toward Diran Base Camp.

  • Days: 4 – 5
  • Route: Tagafari to Base Camp
  • Terrain: Meadows, canyon trails & glacier crossing

Tagafari (3,700m)

Reach the scenic ridge of Tagafari where stunning views of Rakaposhi and Diran dominate the skyline.

  • Elevation: 3,700m
  • Highlights: Rakaposhi & Diran views

Diran Base Camp (3,900m)

Cross the Minapin Glacier and establish Base Camp beneath the icy slopes of Diran Peak.

  • Elevation: 3,900m
  • Terrain: Glacier crossing

Phase 3: Acclimatization & Climbing

The main climbing period begins with acclimatization rotations, ice climbing, and summit preparation.

  • Days: 6 – 35
  • Focus: Camp I & Camp II setup
  • Support: Full Base Camp logistics & meals

Phase 4: The Return Journey

Descend back through Tagafari and Minapin before returning to Islamabad for departure.

  • Days: 36 – 40
  • Route: Base Camp to Islamabad

Essential Travel Tips

Proper preparation and flexibility are important for a successful high-altitude expedition in the Karakoram.

  • Sustainable Gear: Use eco-friendly equipment & solar chargers
  • Mental Flexibility: Be prepared for weather delays
  • Hydration: Maintain high calorie & fluid intake
