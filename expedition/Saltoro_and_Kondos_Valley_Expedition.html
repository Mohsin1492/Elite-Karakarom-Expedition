Saltoro & Kondus Valley Expedition

The Hidden Granite Frontier

The Saltoro and Kondus Valley Expeditions take you into one of the most remote and technically demanding climbing regions in the Karakoram. These valleys, once closed for decades, are now open for elite mountaineering exploration.

Known for their untouched granite spires and massive alpine peaks, this region offers a rare opportunity to climb in complete wilderness with minimal human presence and maximum adventure.

Historical Insight

“Villagers in the Saltoro and Kondus Valleys no longer see expeditions… these eastern valleys contain some of the finest clean granite towers in Baltistan.” — John Mock

Major Peaks of the Region

  • Saltoro Kangri I (7,742m): Highest peak of the Saltoro Range.
  • Saltoro Kangri II (7,705m): A massive snow-covered summit.
  • Sherpi Kangri I (7,380m): Highly technical alpine climb.
  • Sherpi Kangri II (7,100m): Remote and challenging peak.
  • Nearby Giants: Link Sar, K6, K7, and Chogolisa.

Expedition Experience

This region is ideal for experienced climbers seeking true alpine-style expeditions. The terrain includes steep granite faces, mixed snow routes, and unexplored ridgelines.

Many sub-6,000m peaks remain unnamed and unclimbed, offering first ascent opportunities for modern mountaineers.

Why Climb Here?

  • Remote and untouched climbing environment
  • Access to world-class granite formations
  • Reduced approach times from road-accessible routes
  • Opportunities for first ascents
  • Ideal for advanced alpine-style expeditions

Final Note

The Saltoro and Kondus Valleys represent the final frontier of Karakoram climbing. With limited historical exploration and vast untouched terrain, this region is a dream destination for elite alpinists.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Islamabad where our staff welcomes you at the airport and transfers you to the hotel. Meet the expedition team, complete equipment checks, and attend a briefing session.

  • Activity: Airport transfer & expedition briefing
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Dinner

Day 2 – Fly to Skardu / Drive to Chilas

Take a scenic mountain flight to Skardu or travel by road along the Karakoram Highway beside the Indus River.

  • Travel: Flight or Karakoram Highway drive
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 – Skardu Preparation Day

Finalize expedition permits, purchase last-minute supplies, and attend the mandatory tourism department briefing.

  • Activity: Official briefing & logistics
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4 – Drive to Khorkondo

Drive deep into the eastern valleys toward the remote village of Khorkondo and establish the first expedition camp.

  • Drive: 4×4 Jeep Journey
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5 – Trek to Khorkondo Base Camp

Approach the Khorkondo Base Camp surrounded by unnamed granite towers and untouched alpine terrain.

  • Trek: Glacier approach
  • Stay: Full-service Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 to 10 – Alpine Climbing Period

Five days dedicated to alpine-style climbing on unclimbed peaks, granite spires, and ice pinnacles in one of the least explored regions of the Karakoram.

  • Activity: Technical climbing & exploration
  • Stay: Base Camp / High Camps
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 11 – Return to Khorkondo

Descend from Base Camp back toward Khorkondo and prepare for the next stage of the expedition.

  • Trek: Descent to village
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 12 – Transfer to Kebri Glacier

Move toward the Kebri Glacier region, known for its massive granite walls and technical alpine routes.

  • Travel: Jeep & trekking approach
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 13 to 22 – Kebri Glacier Climbing Period

Ten days reserved for technical climbing objectives around the Kebri Glacier, including alpine ascents, granite wall climbing, and exploration of unclimbed routes.

  • Activity: Big wall climbing & summit attempts
  • Stay: Base Camp / High Camps
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 23 – Return to Skardu

Return to Skardu for rest and the official expedition debriefing with tourism authorities.

  • Travel: Drive back to Skardu
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 24 – Journey Toward Islamabad / Chilas

Begin the return journey to Islamabad either by flight or via the Karakoram Highway.

  • Travel: Flight or road transfer
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 25 – Continue to Islamabad

Arrive back in Islamabad and enjoy free time for rest or sightseeing.

  • Travel: Road or flight completion
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 26 – Contingency / City Tour

Reserved for possible delays or optional exploration of Islamabad’s landmarks and markets.

  • Activity: Flexible schedule
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 27 – Departure

Transfer to Islamabad International Airport for your onward international flight, concluding the expedition.

  • Activity: Airport transfer
  • Meals: Breakfast
