Spantik Expedition 2026

Your First 7,000m Summit in the Karakoram

Spantik Peak rises to 7,027 meters and is one of the most accessible high-altitude climbs in Pakistan. Known as the “Golden Peak,” it glows beautifully during sunrise and sunset, making it one of the most scenic expeditions in the Karakoram.

This expedition is ideal for climbers preparing for 8,000m peaks or those seeking their first major mountaineering achievement.

Summer 2026 Spantik Peak Open

Expedition Snapshot

  • Mountain: Spantik (Golden Peak)
  • Elevation: 7,027m
  • Location: Shigar Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Duration: 30–35 Days
  • Route: Southeast Ridge
  • Difficulty: Moderate to High

Expedition Highlights

  • Summit the famous “Golden Peak”
  • Perfect introduction to 7,000m climbing
  • Scenic flight over Karakoram peaks
  • Explore Shigar Valley and Baltistan culture
  • Panoramic views of Rakaposhi, Diran, and surrounding ranges

About the Expedition

Spantik is often considered the best training peak for climbers aiming to step into higher expeditions. While technically less demanding than 8,000m peaks, it still requires strong endurance and proper acclimatization.

The route follows a long ridge with snow slopes ranging from 30° to 45°, making it ideal for developing mountaineering skills.

The Journey

The expedition begins in Islamabad followed by a flight to Skardu. From there, a jeep ride leads to Arandu village, the starting point of the trek.

The trek continues through remote valleys and glaciers to reach Spantik Base Camp, surrounded by stunning mountain scenery.

Climbing Route & Camps

  • Base Camp: ~4,300m
  • Camp I: ~5,100m
  • Camp II: ~5,500m
  • Camp III: ~6,500m
  • Summit: 7,027m via Southeast Ridge

Final Note

Spantik is more than just a climb—it is a gateway into the world of high-altitude mountaineering. It builds confidence, skill, and unforgettable memories in the heart of the Karakoram.

Spantik Expedition Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive in Islamabad and transfer to the hotel. In the evening, the team gathers for an expedition briefing and gear inspection.

  • Stay: Hotel
  • Activity: Gear check & orientation

Day 2: Flight to Skardu (2,230m)

Take the scenic flight to Skardu with spectacular aerial views of the Karakoram giants, including Nanga Parbat.

  • Flight: 45 Minutes
  • Contingency: Karakoram Highway drive if flight is cancelled

Day 3: Acclimatization in Skardu

Explore the historic sites of Skardu while allowing your body to adapt to the altitude.

  • Highlights: Kharpocho Fort & Manthal Buddha Rock
  • Purpose: Acclimatization

Day 4: Skardu to Arandu (2,950m)

A rugged jeep drive through remote valleys leads to Arandu, the last settlement before the glacier approach.

  • Elevation: 2,950m
  • Highlight: Chogo Lungma Glacier region

Day 5: Trek to Bukhum

Begin trekking along the Basha River and onto the Chogo Lungma Glacier toward Bukhum campsite.

  • Trek: 6 Hours
  • Terrain: Glacier moraine & alpine pasture

Day 6: Trek to Khurumal

Continue through glacial terrain as the massive pyramid of Spantik Peak comes into full view.

  • Highlight: First full view of Spantik Peak
  • Terrain: Moraine & high-altitude meadows

Day 7: Bolucho Camp (3,900m)

Trek to Bolucho camp near the glacier base while enjoying stunning views of Laila Peak.

  • Elevation: 3,900m
  • Highlight: Views of Laila Peak

Day 8: Arrival at Spantik Base Camp (4,300m)

Reach Spantik Base Camp, your expedition headquarters for the upcoming climbing period.

  • Elevation: 4,300m
  • Stay: Full-Service Base Camp

Day 9 – 24

The main climbing phase includes acclimatization rotations, high camp setup, and the summit push to Spantik Peak.

  • Camp 1: 5,100m
  • Camp 2: 5,500m
  • Camp 3: 6,200m

Summit Push

The final ascent follows a long snow ridge to the summit of Spantik, rewarding climbers with panoramic Karakoram views.

  • Summit: 7,027m
  • Views: Rakaposhi-Haramosh Range

Day: 25 – 31

Descend through Bolucho, Khurumal, and Arandu before returning to Skardu and Islamabad.

  • Return Route: Chogo Lungma Glacier to Islamabad
