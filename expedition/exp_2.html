Charakusa Valley

The Granite Heart of the Karakoram

The Charakusa Valley Expedition takes you into one of the most dramatic climbing regions in Pakistan. Known for its massive granite walls and untouched alpine environment, this valley is a paradise for rock climbers and mountaineers.

Located near Hushe village in Baltistan, Charakusa offers quick access to world-class peaks without long logistical approaches, making it ideal for both technical climbers and experienced trekkers.

Why Choose Charakusa Valley?

  • Budget-Friendly Expeditions: Many peaks below 6,000m require no peak permits or liaison officers.
  • Easy Access: Short approach trek of around 3 days from Hushe village.
  • Scenic Base Camps: Green meadows surrounded by granite towers and glaciers.
  • World-Class Climbing: Technical routes suitable for advanced climbers.

Notable Peaks in Charakusa Valley

  • K6 (7,281m): A massive and dominant ice and rock formation.
  • K7 (6,934m): Famous for its steep granite walls and technical routes.
  • Link Sar (7,041m): A highly challenging peak for elite climbers.
  • Drifika (6,650m): A sharp and beautiful snow pyramid.
  • Niza Peak (<6,000m): Ideal for accessible, no-permit climbs.

Approach & Trekking Route

The journey begins in Hushe, a small but important village in Baltistan known as the gateway to many Karakoram expeditions. From here, a scenic trek leads toward Charakusa Valley.

The route passes through alpine pastures, river crossings, and glacier moraines before reaching base camps surrounded by towering granite peaks and snow-covered summits.

Expedition Experience

Charakusa Valley offers a unique combination of technical climbing and wilderness exploration. Climbers can experience both rock and ice routes in a remote environment with minimal human presence.
