Saltoro & Kondus Valley Expedition

The Hidden Granite Frontier

The Saltoro and Kondus Valley Expeditions take you into one of the most remote and technically demanding climbing regions in the Karakoram. These valleys, once closed for decades, are now open for elite mountaineering exploration.

Known for their untouched granite spires and massive alpine peaks, this region offers a rare opportunity to climb in complete wilderness with minimal human presence and maximum adventure.

Historical Insight

“Villagers in the Saltoro and Kondus Valleys no longer see expeditions… these eastern valleys contain some of the finest clean granite towers in Baltistan.” — John Mock

Major Peaks of the Region

  • Saltoro Kangri I (7,742m): Highest peak of the Saltoro Range.
  • Saltoro Kangri II (7,705m): A massive snow-covered summit.
  • Sherpi Kangri I (7,380m): Highly technical alpine climb.
  • Sherpi Kangri II (7,100m): Remote and challenging peak.
  • Nearby Giants: Link Sar, K6, K7, and Chogolisa.

Expedition Experience

This region is ideal for experienced climbers seeking true alpine-style expeditions. The terrain includes steep granite faces, mixed snow routes, and unexplored ridgelines.

Many sub-6,000m peaks remain unnamed and unclimbed, offering first ascent opportunities for modern mountaineers.

Why Climb Here?

  • Remote and untouched climbing environment
  • Access to world-class granite formations
  • Reduced approach times from road-accessible routes
  • Opportunities for first ascents
  • Ideal for advanced alpine-style expeditions

Final Note

The Saltoro and Kondus Valleys represent the final frontier of Karakoram climbing. With limited historical exploration and vast untouched terrain, this region is a dream destination for elite alpinists.
