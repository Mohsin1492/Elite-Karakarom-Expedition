Gasherbrum II Expedition 2026

Your First Step Above 8,000m

Gasherbrum II is one of the most popular entry-level 8,000-meter peaks, offering a balanced combination of challenge and accessibility. Standing at 8,035 meters, it is the 13th highest mountain in the world.

This expedition takes climbers deep into the Karakoram, following the famous Baltoro Glacier route to Concordia and onward to Gasherbrum Base Camp.

Expedition Snapshot

  • Mountain: Gasherbrum II (K4)
  • Elevation: 8,035m
  • Ranking: 13th highest mountain
  • Location: Karakoram, Pakistan
  • Duration: 45–50 Days
  • Difficulty: Advanced (High Altitude)
  • Best Season: June – August
  • Base Camp: ~5,200m

Why Climb Gasherbrum II?

  • Ideal introduction to 8,000m mountaineering
  • Less technical compared to K2
  • Incredible Baltoro Glacier trekking route
  • Views of K2, Broad Peak & Gasherbrum I
  • High success rate compared to other peaks

Journey to Base Camp

The expedition begins in Islamabad, followed by a scenic flight to Skardu. After preparation, a jeep ride leads to Askole, the starting point of the trek.

The route follows the Braldu River and continues onto the Baltoro Glacier, passing through iconic camps like Paiju, Urdukas, and Goro II before reaching Concordia.

From Concordia, the journey continues to Gasherbrum Base Camp, surrounded by towering peaks and vast glaciers.

Climbing Route & Camps

  • Base Camp: 5,200m (shared with Gasherbrum I)
  • Camp I: 5,900m via icefall
  • Camp II: 6,400m (Banana Ridge)
  • Camp III: 7,200m summit staging point
  • Summit: 8,035m with panoramic Karakoram views

Expedition Overview (45 Days)

  • Days 1–4: Islamabad → Skardu → Askole
  • Days 5–12: Trek via Baltoro Glacier to Concordia
  • Days 13–37: Climbing rotations & summit attempt
  • Days 38–45: Return to Islamabad

Why Choose Us?

  • Experienced local guides from Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Full logistical support from Islamabad to Base Camp
  • Focus on safety, acclimatization, and success rate
  • Responsible and sustainable expedition practices

Final Note

Gasherbrum II offers a rare opportunity to step into the world of extreme altitude climbing. With the right preparation and support, it becomes a life-changing achievement for any mountaineer.
