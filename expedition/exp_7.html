Laila Peak Expedition 2026

Climb the Iconic Needle of the Karakoram

Laila Peak stands at 6,096 meters and is widely known for its perfectly symmetrical shape and steep snow-covered slopes. It is one of the most beautiful and technically rewarding peaks in Pakistan.

This expedition offers climbers a chance to test their technical skills in a high-altitude environment while enjoying breathtaking views of the Karakoram’s legendary peaks.

Expedition Highlights

  • Climb one of the most iconic peaks in the Karakoram
  • Panoramic views of K2, Broad Peak, and Gasherbrum range
  • Experience the remote Hushe Valley
  • Scenic flight over Nanga Parbat
  • Perfect training climb before 7,000m peaks

Expedition Overview

Laila Peak is often considered a bridge between intermediate and advanced mountaineering. Its steep slopes require strong crampon technique, ice axe control, and high-altitude endurance.

The expedition duration ranges from 23 to 35 days depending on weather conditions and acclimatization progress.

The Journey

The expedition begins in Islamabad, followed by a flight to Skardu where final preparations are completed. From Skardu, a scenic drive leads to Hushe Valley, the gateway to many Karakoram expeditions.

The trek continues through Saicho and onto Laila Peak Base Camp, located at approximately 4,535 meters, surrounded by stunning alpine scenery.

The Climb

The ascent involves steep snow and ice slopes, typically around 40–45 degrees. Climbers must be comfortable using crampons, ropes, and ice tools in exposed conditions.

Our team provides full logistical support at Base Camp, including food, communication, and weather updates, while climbers execute the ascent.

Expedition Overview (23 Days)

  • Days 1–3: Arrival in Islamabad & flight to Skardu
  • Days 4–6: Drive to Hushe & trek to Base Camp
  • Days 7–17: Climbing window & summit attempt
  • Days 18–20: Return trek to Hushe & Skardu
  • Days 21–23: Return to Islamabad & departure

Final Note

Laila Peak is not just a climb—it is a test of precision, balance, and confidence on steep terrain. It prepares climbers for bigger objectives while offering one of the most visually stunning summits in the world.
