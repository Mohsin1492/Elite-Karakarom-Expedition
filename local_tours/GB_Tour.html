Hunza & Skardu Overland Tour (12 Days)

The Complete Northern Pakistan Road Adventure

This 12-day overland journey offers a complete exploration of Northern Pakistan, covering Skardu, Shigar, Khaplu, Hunza, and Gilgit. Traveling by road allows you to fully experience the changing landscapes—from lush valleys to high mountain passes.

Tour Snapshot

  • Duration: 12 Days
  • Mode of Transport: Land Travel
  • Destinations: Naran, Skardu, Shigar, Khaplu, Hunza, Gilgit
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Tour Highlights

  • Scenic drive via Babusar Pass and Lulusar Lake
  • Explore Deosai Plains and Sadpara Lake
  • Visit Shangrila Resort, Shigar & Khaplu Forts
  • Discover Hunza Valley, Attabad Lake & Passu Cones
  • Visit Khunjerab Pass – highest road border
  • Explore Gilgit city and ancient Silk Route

About the Tour

This overland tour is perfect for travelers who want to experience the true essence of Northern Pakistan. Unlike flight-based tours, this journey allows you to witness dramatic landscape changes and enjoy scenic road trips.

From cultural heritage sites to high-altitude plateaus, this tour covers the most iconic destinations in Gilgit-Baltistan.

The Journey

Starting from Islamabad, the journey passes through Naran and Babusar Pass before reaching Skardu. After exploring Skardu, Shigar, and Khaplu, the route continues to Hunza and Khunjerab Pass.

The return journey includes Gilgit and scenic drives back through the mountains, completing a full northern circuit.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy long scenic drives, sightseeing, cultural exploration, photography, light hiking, and relaxation in some of the most beautiful valleys in the world.

Optional activities include hiking to forts, visiting local markets, and experiencing traditional village life.

Detailed Itinerary (12 Days)

  • Day 1: Islamabad → Naran → Hotel check-in → Free time → Night stay in Naran
  • Day 2: Drive via Babusar Pass → Lulusar Lake → Arrival in Shigar → Night stay
  • Day 3: Visit Deosai Plains → Sadpara Lake → Night stay in Shigar
  • Day 4: Visit Shangrila Resort → Upper Kachura Lake → Skardu Bazaar → Optional Kharpocho Fort hike → Night stay in Shigar
  • Day 5: Drive to Khaplu → Visit Sailing Village, Chaqchan Mosque & Khaplu Fort → Night stay in Khaplu
  • Day 6: Drive to Hunza → Arrival & rest → Night stay in Karimabad
  • Day 7: Visit Baltit Fort → Hopper Glacier → Altit Fort → Sunset at Duikar → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 8: Visit Attabad Lake → Passu → Khunjerab Pass → Return → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 9: Free day in Hunza → Optional café & shopping → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 10: Drive to Gilgit → Rakaposhi View Point → Visit Bazaar & Buddha Rock → Night stay in Gilgit
  • Day 11: Drive to Naran/Besham → Night stay
  • Day 12: Return to Islamabad → Drop-off
