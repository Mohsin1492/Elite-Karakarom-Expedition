Hunza Valley Tour (6 Days)

The Land of Legends – Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley is one of the most popular travel destinations in the world, located in the far northern region of Pakistan. Often referred to as the “Land of Legends,” it is famous for its breathtaking landscapes, rich culture, and warm hospitality.

Surrounded by the mighty Karakoram Range, the valley offers stunning views of peaks like Rakaposhi, Ultar, and Spantik. From lush valleys to rugged mountains, this journey provides a complete northern experience.

  • Scenic drive through Kaghan Valley and Babusar Pass

About the Tour

This tour is designed to provide a complete experience of Hunza Valley, combining scenic beauty, cultural heritage, and historical exploration.

Travelers will explore ancient forts, interact with local communities, and enjoy breathtaking landscapes throughout the journey.

The Journey

The journey starts from Islamabad and passes through the scenic Kaghan Valley to reach Hunza. Along the way, travelers experience dramatic changes in landscape—from lush green valleys to rugged mountain terrain.

The tour includes visits to Khunjerab Pass, historical forts, lakes, and local markets before returning to Islamabad.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy sightseeing, cultural tours, photography, shopping, and interaction with local guides who share the history and traditions of Hunza Valley.

Travelers can also enjoy local cuisine, visit cafés, and explore traditional villages.

Detailed Itinerary (6 Days)

  • Day 1: Islamabad → Naran → Scenic drive via Kaghan Valley → Free time → Night stay in Naran
  • Day 2: Naran → Lulusar Lake → Babusar Pass → Karakoram Highway → Rakaposhi View Point → Arrival in Hunza
  • Day 3: Visit Baltit Fort → Altit Fort → Royal Gardens → Duikar sunset → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 4: Visit Attabad Lake → Passu Cones → Khunjerab Pass → Shopping in Sost & Karimabad → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 5: Return to Naran → Stops along the way → Visit Naran Bazaar → Night stay in Naran
  • Day 6: Return to Islamabad → Drop-off
