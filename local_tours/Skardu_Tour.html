Skardu Tour (7 Days)

The Ultimate Road Journey to Skardu

This 7-day Skardu tour package is designed for travelers who want to experience the beauty of Northern Pakistan through a scenic road trip. The journey covers the Himalayan and Karakoram mountain ranges, offering breathtaking landscapes, rivers, lakes, and valleys along the way.

Organized with professional local guides, this tour ensures a safe, comfortable, and unforgettable travel experience across Gilgit-Baltistan.

About the Tour

This tour offers a complete road journey experience to Skardu, passing through some of the most scenic routes in the world. It is ideal for travelers who enjoy long drives, natural landscapes, and cultural exploration.

The package includes visits to historical sites, lakes, deserts, and high-altitude plateaus, making it a comprehensive Northern Pakistan experience.

The Journey

The journey begins from Islamabad and passes through Balakot, Mansehra, Babusar Pass, and Chilas before reaching Skardu via the Indus River.

The return journey follows a scenic route through Naran or Besham, completing a full northern circuit.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy long scenic drives, photography stops, cultural visits, light hiking, and exploration of valleys, lakes, and historical landmarks.

Optional activities include hiking to Kharpocho Fort and exploring local villages and bazaars.

Detailed Itinerary (7 Days)

  • Day 1: Islamabad → Balakot/Mansehra → Babusar Pass → Lulusar Lake → Chilas → Night stay in Chilas
  • Day 2: Chilas → Indus River Drive → Alam Bridge → Arrival in Skardu → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 3: Shangrila Resort → Upper Kachura Lake → Shigar → Cold Desert → Shigar Fort → Optional Kharpocho Fort hike → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 4: Ghanche District → Sailing Village → Khaplu Fort → Chaqchan Mosque → Manthoka Waterfall → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 5: Deosai Plains → Sadpara Lake → Sheosar Lake → Bara Pani → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 6: Skardu → Naran/Besham → Night stay
  • Day 7: Return to Islamabad → Drop-off
