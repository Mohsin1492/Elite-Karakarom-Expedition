Skardu Tour (6 Days)

Explore Skardu with Culture, Comfort & Adventure

This 6-day Skardu tour offers a more relaxed and immersive experience, allowing travelers to explore Baltistan’s natural beauty, cultural heritage, and scenic landscapes at a comfortable pace.

Tour Availability 2026

Date Tour Status
Summer 2026 Skardu Premium Tour Open

Tour Snapshot

  • Duration: 6 Days
  • Mode of Transport: Air Travel (Flight)
  • Destinations: Skardu, Shigar, Khaplu, Deosai
  • Difficulty: Easy

Tour Highlights

  • Visit Shangrila Resort and Upper Kachura Lake
  • Explore Cold Desert and Shigar Valley
  • Optional hike to Jarba Xho viewpoint
  • Experience Deosai Plains and Sadpara Lake
  • Visit Khaplu Fort and Chaqchan Mosque
  • Trip to Manthoka Waterfall
  • Optional hike to Kharpocho Fort

About the Tour

This extended Skardu tour provides more time to explore and enjoy each destination without rushing. It combines sightseeing, cultural visits, and optional hiking experiences.

Ideal for travelers who prefer a relaxed itinerary with additional exploration opportunities.

The Journey

The journey begins with a flight to Skardu, followed by visits to Shangrila Resort, Shigar Valley, and Deosai Plains. Travelers will explore surrounding districts including Ghanche and Kharmang.

The extended duration allows for optional hikes and deeper cultural experiences.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy sightseeing, cultural exploration, photography, and optional hiking experiences such as Jarba Xho and Kharpocho Fort.

The tour also offers time for relaxation and exploring local markets.

Detailed Itinerary (6 Days)

  • Day 1: Flight to Skardu → Visit Shangrila Resort → Upper Kachura Lake → Optional Xokh Nala → Hotel check-in → City/Bazaar visit → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 2: Drive to Shigar → Visit Shigar Fort → Optional walk & Jarba Xho hike → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 3: Visit Deosai Plains → Sadpara Lake → Return → Optional Kharpocho Fort hike → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 4: Visit Ghanche District → Sailing Village → Khaplu Fort → Chaqchan Mosque → Night stay in Khaplu/Skardu
  • Day 5: Visit Kharmang District → Manthoka Waterfall → Return → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 6: Drop at Skardu Airport → Flight to Islamabad

Why Choose This Tour?

  • Relaxed and extended itinerary
  • More time for exploration and activities
  • Optional hiking experiences included
  • Comfortable and well-organized tour
  • Perfect for leisure travelers

Final Note

This tour is ideal for those who want to explore Skardu at a comfortable pace while enjoying both adventure and relaxation. A perfect balance of nature, culture, and experience.
