Skardu Tour (5 Days)

Discover the Beauty of Skardu & Surroundings

This 5-day Skardu tour offers a perfect short getaway to explore the stunning landscapes of Baltistan. From lakes and deserts to forts and waterfalls, this journey covers the most iconic attractions of Skardu.

Tour Availability 2026

Date Tour Status
Summer 2026 Skardu Tour Open

Tour Snapshot

  • Duration: 5 Days
  • Mode of Transport: Air Travel (Flight)
  • Destinations: Skardu, Shigar, Khaplu, Deosai
  • Difficulty: Easy

Tour Highlights

  • Visit Shangrila Resort and Upper Kachura Lake
  • Explore Deosai Plains and Sadpara Lake
  • Experience Cold Desert and Shigar Fort
  • Visit Khaplu Fort and Chaqchan Mosque
  • Trip to Manthoka Waterfall
  • Optional hike to Kharpocho Fort

About the Tour

This short tour is ideal for travelers who want to explore Skardu in a limited time. It combines natural beauty, cultural heritage, and relaxing experiences into one compact itinerary.

With comfortable travel arrangements and guided visits, this tour is suitable for families, couples, and solo travelers.

The Journey

The journey begins with a flight from Islamabad to Skardu, followed by visits to famous attractions including Shangrila Resort, Deosai Plains, and surrounding valleys.

Travelers will explore Shigar, Khaplu, and Kharmang districts before returning to Islamabad.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy sightseeing, cultural visits, photography, light hiking, and relaxation in beautiful landscapes.

Optional activities include hiking to Kharpocho Fort and exploring local bazaars.

Detailed Itinerary (5 Days)

  • Day 1: Flight to Skardu → Visit Shangrila Resort → Upper Kachura Lake → Optional Xokh Nala → Hotel check-in → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 2: Visit Deosai Plains → Sadpara Lake → Return to Skardu → Cold Desert → Shigar Fort → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 3: Visit Ghanche District → Sailing Village → Khaplu Fort → Chaqchan Mosque → Night stay in Khaplu/Skardu
  • Day 4: Visit Kharmang District → Manthoka Waterfall → Return → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 5: Drop at Skardu Airport → Flight to Islamabad
