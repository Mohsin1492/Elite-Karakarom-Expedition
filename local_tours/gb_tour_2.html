Hunza, Fairy Meadows & Skardu Tour

The Ultimate Northern Pakistan Experience

This 7-day tour combines the cultural beauty of Hunza, the magical landscapes of Fairy Meadows, and the breathtaking valleys of Skardu. It is a perfect journey for travelers who want a mix of adventure, nature, and iconic mountain views.

Tour Snapshot

  • Duration: 7 Days
  • Mode of Transport: Air Travel (Flight)
  • Destinations: Hunza, Fairy Meadows, Skardu
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate (with light hiking)

Tour Highlights

  • Explore Baltit and Altit Forts in Hunza
  • Sunset at Duikar (Eagle’s Nest)
  • Visit Attabad Lake and Passu Cones
  • Experience Khunjerab Pass – highest road border
  • Adventure trip to Fairy Meadows with jeep ride & hike
  • Views of Nanga Parbat from Fairy Meadows
  • Explore Deosai Plains, Shangrila & Shigar Fort

About the Tour

This tour is designed for travelers who want to experience the best of Northern Pakistan in a short time. It blends cultural heritage, scenic landscapes, and light adventure into one unforgettable journey.

From the peaceful valleys of Hunza to the adventurous trails of Fairy Meadows and the majestic beauty of Skardu, every destination offers something unique.

The Journey

The tour begins with a flight to Gilgit followed by a scenic drive to Hunza. After exploring the region and visiting Khunjerab Pass, the journey continues towards Fairy Meadows via Raikot Bridge.

After experiencing the beauty of Nanga Parbat, the tour proceeds to Skardu, where travelers explore lakes, deserts, and cultural landmarks.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy sightseeing, cultural visits, jeep adventures, hiking to Fairy Meadows, photography, and relaxing in nature.

The Fairy Meadows hike and jeep ride provide an exciting adventure, making this tour perfect for both nature lovers and thrill seekers.

Detailed Itinerary (7 Days)

  • Day 1: Flight to Gilgit → Drive to Hunza → Visit Baltit & Altit Fort → Sunset at Duikar → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 2: Visit Attabad Lake → Passu Cones → Khunjerab Pass → Return via Sost → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 3: Drive to Raikot → Jeep ride to Tatu → Hike to Fairy Meadows → Night stay at huts
  • Day 4: Sunrise at Fairy Meadows → Return to Raikot → Drive to Skardu → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 5: Visit Deosai Plains → Sadpara Lake → Optional Kharpocho Fort hike → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 6: Visit Shangrila Resort → Upper Kachura Lake → Cold Desert → Shigar Fort → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 7: Drop at Skardu Airport → Flight to Islamabad
