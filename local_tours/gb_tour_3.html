Hunza & Skardu Tour

Explore the Beauty of Hunza & Skardu

Experience the breathtaking landscapes of Gilgit-Baltistan with this 7-day tour covering Hunza and Skardu. From historical forts and serene lakes to the world’s highest border at Khunjerab Pass, this journey offers a perfect blend of culture, adventure, and natural beauty.

Tour Snapshot

  • Duration: 7 Days
  • Mode of Transport: Air Travel (Flight)
  • Destinations: Hunza, Khunjerab Pass, Skardu
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Tour Highlights

  • Visit Baltit Fort and Altit Fort in Hunza
  • Sunset view from Duikar (Eagle’s Nest)
  • Explore Attabad Lake and Passu Cones
  • Visit Khunjerab Pass – highest road border in the world
  • Witness Deosai Plains and Sadpara Lake
  • Explore Shangrila Resort, Shigar Fort & Cold Desert

About the Tour

This tour is designed for travelers who want to explore the most iconic destinations of Northern Pakistan in a short time. From the cultural richness of Hunza to the vast landscapes of Skardu, each day brings a new experience.

With comfortable travel arrangements and guided visits, this journey is ideal for families, groups, and adventure seekers.

The Journey

The journey begins with a flight from Islamabad to Gilgit, followed by a scenic drive to Hunza. Travelers explore historical sites, lakes, glaciers, and the famous Khunjerab Pass before heading towards Skardu.

In Skardu, the tour continues with visits to Deosai Plains, lakes, deserts, and cultural heritage sites, offering a complete northern experience.

Activities & Experience

Enjoy sightseeing, cultural exploration, light trekking, photography, and relaxation in some of the most beautiful landscapes in the world.

Optional activities include hiking to Kharpocho Fort and visiting local cafés and markets.

Detailed Itinerary (7 Days)

  • Day 1: Flight to Gilgit → Drive to Hunza → Visit Baltit & Altit Fort → Sunset at Duikar → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 2: Visit Attabad Lake → Passu Cones → Khunjerab Pass → Return via Sost → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 3: Visit Nagar → Hopper Glacier → Free time in Karimabad → Night stay in Hunza
  • Day 4: Drive to Skardu via Indus River → Hotel check-in → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 5: Visit Deosai Plains → Sadpara Lake → Optional Kharpocho Fort hike → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 6: Visit Shangrila Resort → Upper Kachura Lake → Cold Desert → Shigar Fort → Night stay in Skardu
  • Day 7: Drop at Skardu Airport → Flight to Islamabad
