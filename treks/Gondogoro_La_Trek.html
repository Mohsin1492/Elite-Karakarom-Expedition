Gondogoro La Trek

The Ultimate High-Altitude Circuit

The Gondogoro La Trek is one of the most thrilling and complete trekking experiences in the Karakoram Range. This journey takes you deep into the world of towering peaks, massive glaciers, and remote valleys, combining both adventure and cultural discovery.

Starting from the Baltoro Glacier, trekkers reach Concordia — a legendary location surrounded by some of the highest mountains on Earth. The journey continues with the challenging crossing of Gondogoro La (5,585m), offering one of the most breathtaking panoramic views in the world.

This trek is not just about landscapes; it’s about experiencing the raw beauty of nature, the strength of high-altitude adventure, and the unique culture of the Baltistan region.

  • Concordia: The famous “Throne Room of the Mountains”.
  • Base Camps: Visit K2 and Broad Peak base camps.

  • Gilkey Memorial: Tribute to climbers near K2.

Trek Experience

The journey begins in Islamabad before flying to Skardu, the gateway to the Karakoram. From there, a jeep ride leads to Askole, the last village before entering the wilderness.

Trekkers follow the Baltoro Glacier through dramatic landscapes toward Concordia, where multiple giant peaks surround you in every direction. The adventure then continues with the challenging crossing of Gondogoro La.

After crossing the pass, the trail descends into the peaceful Hushe Valley, where you experience a completely different landscape and local culture.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Islamabad

Welcome to Pakistan! Most international flights arrive in Islamabad early in the morning. Our team will receive you at the airport and transfer you to your hotel.

The rest of the day is free for rest after your journey. Once all participants arrive, we will arrange a short briefing session to introduce the trek plan, safety guidelines, and answer your questions. If time allows, you may also explore Islamabad, a clean and modern capital city.

Day 2 – Flight to Skardu (2,230m)

Today we take a scenic flight to Skardu, the gateway to the Karakoram. This short flight offers breathtaking aerial views of the Himalayan and Karakoram ranges, including Nanga Parbat.

After landing, transfer to your hotel. You can spend the day visiting local markets, lakes, or historical places like Buddha Rock.

Note: Flights depend on weather. If cancelled, we travel by road via Karakoram Highway with an overnight stop.

Day 3 – Skardu Exploration & Preparation

This day is important for acclimatization and final preparations.

Optional visits:

  • Kharpocho Fort (panoramic view of Skardu)
  • Manthal Buddha Rock
  • Local bazaars and lakes

Meanwhile, our team completes permits and logistics.

Day 4 – Drive to Askoli (3,100m)

We drive along the Indus, Shigar, and Braldu rivers toward Askoli — the last village before entering the wilderness.

Meet your porter team here. Depending on conditions, we continue toward Johla.

Day 5 – Trek to Jula (3,150m)

A long but exciting trek begins through the dry terrain of Braldu Gorge. The route passes the Biafo Glacier snout and continues toward Jula campsite.

Expect suspension bridges, rocky trails, and strong sun exposure.

Day 6 – Trek to Paiju (3,380m)

A steady trek along the Braldu River takes you to Paiju Camp — a green campsite with your first clear views of the Trango Towers.

Day 7 – Trek to Khoburtse

Today you step onto the Baltoro Glacier. The terrain becomes rocky and glacial.

Camp at Khoburtse with stunning views of:

  • Trango Towers
  • Cathedral Peak
  • Uli Biaho

Day 8 – Trek to Urdukas

A shorter but steady climb leads to Urdukas, one of the most beautiful camps on the route.

Enjoy panoramic views of the glacier and surrounding peaks.

Day 9 – Acclimatization Day (Urdukas)

A rest day for altitude adjustment.

Activities:

  • Short walks
  • Relaxation
  • Photography

This day is crucial for avoiding altitude sickness.

Day 10 – Trek to Goro II (4,295m)

The environment becomes more dramatic and icy.

Highlights:

  • Views of Masherbrum (Queen of Karakoram)
  • Camping directly on glacier

Day 11 – Trek to Concordia (4,500m)

One of the most unforgettable days.

Concordia is the meeting point of glaciers and offers a 360° view of giant peaks, including:

  • K2
  • Broad Peak
  • Gasherbrum range

Day 12 – Trek to Broad Peak Base Camp

A short trek brings you closer to K2.

Camp at Broad Peak Base Camp with incredible views.

Day 13 – K2 Base Camp Visit & Return

Early morning trek to K2 Base Camp (5,150m).

Visit:

  • Gilkey Memorial
  • Base Camp of the world’s 2nd highest peak

Return to Concordia for overnight stay.

Day 14 – Trek to Ali Camp

Leave the main trail and head toward Gondogoro route.

Camp at Ali Camp and prepare for the pass crossing.

Day 15 – Cross Gondogoro La (5,600m)

The most challenging day.

Start before sunrise and climb using ropes and safety gear.

At the top, enjoy views of:

  • K2
  • Broad Peak
  • Gasherbrum I & II

Descend to Khuspang.

Day 16 – Buffer Day

Reserved for weather delays or rest.

Day 17 – Trek to Saicho

Descend into greener terrain.

Pass:

  • Flower fields
  • Yak pastures

Camp at Saicho.

Day 18 – Trek to Hushe & Drive to Skardu

Short trek to Hushe village.

Then drive back to Skardu via scenic valleys.

Hotel stay and rest.

Day 19 – Return to Islamabad

Fly back to Islamabad (weather permitting).

Free time for:

  • Shopping
  • City visit

Day 20 – Departure

Transfer to airport for your international flight.

End of your unforgettable journey!
