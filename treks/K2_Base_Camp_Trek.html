K2 Base Camp Trek

The Ultimate Karakoram Expedition

The K2 Base Camp Trek is one of the most iconic and challenging adventures in the world, taking you deep into the heart of Pakistan’s Karakoram Range. This extraordinary journey leads you to the base of K2 (8,611m), the second-highest and one of the most demanding mountains on Earth.

Trekkers travel along the legendary Baltoro Glacier, passing through dramatic landscapes surrounded by towering peaks. A major highlight of the journey is Concordia, often called the “Throne Room of the Mountain Gods”, where some of the world’s highest peaks stand together in a breathtaking panorama.

This expedition combines adventure, natural beauty, and cultural experience, offering a complete trekking journey through the stunning region of Baltistan.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Islamabad

Welcome to Pakistan! Your journey begins as you arrive at Islamabad International Airport. Our team will receive you and transfer you to your hotel so you can relax after your flight.

In the evening, you’ll meet your fellow trekkers and enjoy a welcome dinner. We’ll conduct a complete briefing about the K2 Base Camp trek, explain the plan ahead, and answer your questions. Afterward, take a good rest to prepare for the adventure.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 2 – Flight to Skardu (2,230m)

Today you’ll take a scenic flight to Skardu, one of the most beautiful mountain flights in the world. From your window seat, you may witness breathtaking views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram range.

After landing, we transfer you to your hotel where you’ll meet part of your trekking team. You can spend the rest of the day exploring Skardu, the cultural hub of Baltistan.

Note: Flights depend on weather conditions. If canceled, we travel by road via the Karakoram Highway with an overnight stay in Naran.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 – Skardu Sightseeing & Preparation

This day is important for acclimatization and final preparations. While our team arranges permits and logistics, you can explore Skardu’s famous spots.

  • Kharpocho Fort – panoramic views of Skardu Valley
  • Sadpara Lake – a calm and scenic lake
  • Buddha Rock – an ancient carving from early history
  • Kachura Lakes – beautiful upper and lower lakes

Enjoy your last comfortable hotel stay before heading into the mountains.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4 – Drive to Johla (3,075m)

The real adventure begins today with a jeep journey through the Shigar and Braldu valleys. The drive takes around 8 hours and offers dramatic mountain scenery.

We pass Askole, the final village before entering the wilderness. From here onward, you are truly in the remote Karakoram region. We reach Johla and set up our first campsite by the river.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5 – Trek to Paiju (3,420m)

Your first trekking day begins along the Braldu River. The trail is moderate and serves as a good warm-up for the days ahead.

After several hours of walking, we reach Paiju camp, a green campsite surrounded by mountains. From here, you may start seeing the famous Trango Towers.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 – Trek to Khoburtse (3,795m)

Today you step onto the Baltoro Glacier for the first time. This massive glacier becomes your route for the coming days.

The terrain is rocky and uneven, but manageable with careful steps. After crossing sections of the glacier, we reach Khoburtse campsite with stunning views of surrounding peaks.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7 – Trek to Urdukas (3,900m)

A shorter but slightly challenging day as we move along the glacier’s surface. Urdukas is one of the most beautiful camps on this route.

From here, you get incredible views of the Baltoro Glacier along with Cathedral Peak and Trango Towers. It is also one of the last camps with basic facilities.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 8 – Rest & Acclimatization at Urdukas

Today is reserved for acclimatization. At this altitude, your body needs time to adjust before going higher.

You can take short walks around camp or simply relax and enjoy the surrounding scenery. This rest day is essential for a safe and successful trek.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 9 – Trek to Goro II (4,295m)

We continue deeper into the glacier terrain. The environment becomes more rugged and high-altitude conditions are more noticeable.

Masherbrum Peak appears in full view, adding to the beauty of the landscape. Tonight, we camp directly on the glacier.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 10 – Trek to Concordia (4,500m)

One of the most unforgettable days of the journey. Concordia is where multiple glaciers meet, forming a spectacular natural amphitheater.

You will witness several major peaks including Broad Peak and the Gasherbrum group. K2 also becomes visible, standing tall at the end of the valley.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 11 – Trek to Broad Peak Base Camp (4,800m)

We move closer to the high mountains by trekking towards Broad Peak Base Camp. The views of K2 from this area are even more impressive.

This stop prepares us for the final approach to K2 Base Camp.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 12 – K2 Base Camp (5,150m)

Today is the highlight of the entire trek. We reach K2 Base Camp, standing at the foot of the world’s second-highest mountain.

On the way, we visit the Gilkey Memorial, dedicated to climbers who lost their lives. After spending time at base camp, we return to Concordia.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 13 – Return to Goro II

We begin the return journey, retracing our path across the glacier. The scenery looks different on the way back, offering a fresh perspective.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 14 – Trek to Khoburtse

Descending further, we gradually leave the higher glacier sections. The air becomes easier to breathe as we lose altitude.p>

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 15 – Trek to Paiju

We move off the glacier and return to the greener surroundings of Paiju camp. The change in landscape is refreshing after days on ice.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 16 – Trek to Johla

The final trekking day takes us back along the Braldu River. In the evening, we celebrate with our team, appreciating their support throughout the journey.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 17 – Drive to Skardu

We travel back to Skardu by jeep. After days in the wilderness, you’ll enjoy the comfort of a hotel, hot shower, and proper rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 18 – Flight to Islamabad

Return to Islamabad by air. The rest of the day is free for relaxation or shopping.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 19 – Contingency / Free Day

This day is kept as a buffer in case of flight delays. If not needed, you can explore Islamabad or nearby Rawalpindi.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 20 – Departure

After breakfast, we transfer you to the airport for your return flight. You leave with unforgettable memories of the Karakoram.

Meals: Breakfast
