Thalle La Trek

A Scenic & Easy Karakoram Adventure

The Thalle La Trek is a perfect introduction to trekking in the Karakoram. It offers stunning mountain views, peaceful valleys, and cultural experiences, all without the difficulty of extreme high-altitude climbs.

This trek combines short hiking days with comfortable stays and scenic drives, making it ideal for beginners, families, and travelers looking for a relaxed adventure.

Quick Overview

  • Duration: 7–9 Days
  • Trek Duration: 4 Days
  • Max Altitude: 4,572m (Thalle La Pass)
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
  • Best For: Beginners & Families

Journey Overview

The adventure begins in Islamabad, followed by a scenic flight to Skardu. From here, you explore the cultural and natural beauty of Baltistan, including lakes, forts, and local markets.

The journey continues toward Khaplu, where you stay in a historic palace and explore traditional Balti culture. From there, you travel to Thalle village, the starting point of your trek.

Trekking Experience

The trek connects the Khaplu and Shigar valleys through a beautiful four-day route. Along the way, you pass through remote villages, green pastures, and scenic mountain trails.

Crossing the Thalle La Pass (4,572m) offers panoramic views of the Karakoram mountains. The trek ends in Shigar Valley, where you can relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.

Highlights

  • Easy trekking route suitable for beginners
  • Stunning views of Karakoram landscapes
  • Stay at historic Khaplu Palace & Shigar Fort
  • Experience Balti culture and local life
  • Scenic drives along Indus and Shyok rivers

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Islamabad

Arrive at Islamabad International Airport where our team welcomes you and transfers you to the hotel. After all participants arrive, a short briefing covers the trek plan, safety, and equipment. If time permits, enjoy a brief city tour.

  • Activity: Airport pickup & briefing
  • Stay: Standard Hotel
  • Meals: Dinner

Day 2 – Flight to Skardu

Fly to Skardu, enjoying aerial views of major peaks including Nanga Parbat. After arrival, explore the local market and visit Manthal Buddha Rock. (If flight is cancelled, travel via Karakoram Highway.)

  • Travel: Flight (45 min) / Road alternative
  • Highlights: Mountain views, Manthal Buddha Rock
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 – Skardu Exploration

Acclimatization day with a hike to Kharpocho Fort for panoramic views. Later, explore a traditional village and optionally visit Satpara Lake or local markets.

  • Activity: Fort visit & village walk
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4 – Drive to Khaplu

Drive along scenic rivers to Khaplu Valley. Visit the historic Chaqchan Mosque, known for its unique architectural style.

  • Drive: ~3 Hours
  • Highlights: Shyok River, Chaqchan Mosque
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5 – Drive to Thalle & Trek Start

Reach Thalle Valley via jeep and meet the trekking team. Set up camp and take a short acclimatization walk. Expect warm days and cold nights.

  • Drive: 4×4 Jeep
  • Activity: Short hike
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 – Trek to Azalan

Begin trekking through green pastures and grazing lands. Pass shepherd huts and reach Azalan, located at the base of the pass.

  • Trek Time: ~6 Hours
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7 – Cross Thalle La Pass (4,572m)

Climb to the Thalle La Pass and enjoy wide mountain views. Descend into lush meadows and camp at Raseppi.

  • Trek: 6–7 Hours
  • Stay: Camping
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 8 – Drive to Thalle & Trek Start

Descend through green valleys and cross streams before reaching Shigar. Stay at the historic Shigar Fort for a comfortable night.

  • Trek: ~4 Hours
  • Stay: Shigar Fort / Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 9 – Return to Islamabad

Fly back to Islamabad and transfer to your hotel. Free time for rest or shopping. (If flight is cancelled, travel via road.)

  • Travel: Flight / Road backup
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 10 – Contingency / City Tour

Reserved day for delays or sightseeing. Visit Faisal Mosque, Pakistan Monument, or Daman-e-Koh if time allows.

  • Activity: Flexible
  • Stay: Hotel
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 11 – Departure

Transfer to Islamabad Airport for your flight home, marking the end of your journey.

  • Activity: Airport transfer
  • Meals: Breakfast
