Biafo Hispar La Trek

Crossing the Heart of the Karakoram

The Biafo–Hispar La Trek is one of the longest and most adventurous glacier journeys in the world, stretching approximately 120 kilometers through the central Karakoram. This remarkable route connects Baltistan with Hunza, offering a once-in-a-lifetime experience for serious trekkers.

This expedition takes you across an immense glacial system often referred to as part of the “Third Pole,” where vast ice fields, towering peaks, and untouched wilderness create an environment unlike anywhere else.

Expedition Highlights

  • Snow Lake: A massive glacial basin resembling a frozen ocean.
  • Glacier Traverse: Cross Hispar La Pass (5,150m).
  • Famous Peaks: Latok, Ogre (Baintha Brakk), and Kanjut Sar.
  • Cultural Shift: Baltistan to Hunza traditions.

Itinerary Overview

  • Days 1–3: Islamabad & Skardu Travel (2,350m)
  • Days 4–9: Askole, Biafo Glacier, Baintha Lakes (4,400m)
  • Days 10–14: Snow Lake & Hispar La Crossing (5,150m)
  • Days 15–19: Hunza Valley & Return (2,400m)

Trek Information

  • Difficulty: Strenuous
  • Best Time: June – August
  • Gear: Crampons, rope, harness, UV protection
  • Note: Schedule may change due to weather & flights
